Tapering Off GLP-1s: What We Know and What We Don't

Tapering is commonly discussed, but specific taper schedules are not settled by strong clinical evidence. The safer content angle is clinician-led planning plus careful tracking.

Track clinician-directed GLP-1 transitions in Jabbit
No taper instructions: This page does not provide a taper schedule. Do not change medication timing or dose without qualified clinical guidance.

What is known

  • Withdrawal/extension studies show weight regain can happen after stopping GLP-1 therapy in many participants.
  • Obesity is often managed as a chronic condition, so maintenance strategy matters.
  • Specific taper methods are not proven as a universal way to prevent regain.
