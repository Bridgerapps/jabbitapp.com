What Is Maintenance Dosing?

After reaching your target weight, maintenance dosing involves staying on a lower dose of your GLP-1 medication to:

  • Keep hunger and food noise manageable
  • Sustain your new eating habits
  • Prevent weight regain
  • Maintain metabolic improvements
Key Insight: Most people transition to a lower dose than their titration peak. The goal isn't to keep losing—it's to find the lowest dose that keeps you stable.

Common Maintenance Strategies

1. Dose Reduction

Reduce your dose gradually. Common transitions include:

  • 2.4mg → 1.7mg weekly (Wegovy/Zepbound)
  • 15mg → 10mg or 7.5mg (Mounjaro/Zepbound)
  • 2mg → 1mg weekly (Ozempic)

2. Extended Dosing Interval

Some users space out injections:

  • Weekly → Every 10 days
  • Weekly → Every 2 weeks

Note: Always consult your provider before changing intervals.

3. Combination Approach

Lower dose + lifestyle focus = sustainable results. The medication handles the biological hunger signals while you maintain habit changes.

How to Know If You're Ready

Signs you might be ready for maintenance:

  • You've been at or near your goal weight for 2+ months
  • Your weight has been stable (±3 lbs) for at least 4 weeks
  • You've established sustainable eating habits
  • You have a plan for ongoing medication access

The Reality of Weight Regain

Research shows: Approximately 50% of weight is typically regained within one year of stopping GLP-1 medications entirely. Maintenance isn't optional—it's essential for keeping your results.

The good news? Most people find they can maintain on a significantly lower dose than their titration peak. Your body has adapted, and less medication is needed to maintain the new baseline.

Tips for Long-Term Success

  1. Track your weight weekly – Catches small regains before they become big ones
  2. Prioritize protein – Helps maintain muscle mass and satiety
  3. Stay active – Both exercise and daily movement matter
  4. Plan for challenges – Holidays, stress, and life events can trigger regain
  5. Keep your provider in the loop – Regular check-ins help adjust as needed

FAQ: GLP-1 Maintenance

How long do I need to stay on maintenance?
Most experts recommend at least 1-2 years of maintenance to cement habits and stabilize weight. Some people stay on indefinitely.
Will I regain weight if I stop completely?
Studies show most people regain a significant portion of weight within months of stopping. Maintenance (even at a lower dose) dramatically improves long-term success.
Can I switch providers for maintenance?
Yes! Many people switch to more affordable options for maintenance. Just ensure your medical records transfer and a new provider evaluates your current dose.
What if my insurance stopped covering my medication?
Explore compounding pharmacies (legal and often 50-70% cheaper), manufacturer discount programs, or discuss alternatives with your provider.
