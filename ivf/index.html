Track your IVF medications and appointments

Jabbit keeps your clinic’s calendar and reminds you at the times you set. It doesn’t suggest doses or timing. Always follow your clinic’s instructions.

These pages set out what each IVF medicine’s US prescribing information says: what it is, what it’s approved for, how it’s supplied, storage, side effects and warnings. Every fact is cited to the label on DailyMed with its revision date, and each label’s dosing statement appears only as a quote. There are no suggested doses, timings, protocols or mixing instructions: those come from your clinic.

IVF medicines

Menotropins (Menopur)

FSH and LH activity from purified urinary gonadotropins. Approved for multiple follicle development in ART cycles. Storage, side effects and warnings.

Follitropin alfa (Gonal-f, Gonal-f RFF Redi-ject)

Recombinant FSH in a multi-dose vial or a ready-to-use prefilled pen. Approved uses, refrigeration and side effects.

Follitropin beta (Follistim AQ Cartridge)

Recombinant FSH in a ready-to-use cartridge for the Follistim Pen. Approved uses, refrigeration and side effects.

Cetrorelix acetate (Cetrotide)

GnRH antagonist approved to inhibit premature LH surges. Its label says to store it refrigerated.

Ganirelix acetate

GnRH antagonist in a ready-to-use prefilled syringe. The label we checked says to store it at room temperature.

Trigger shot: choriogonadotropin alfa (Ovidrel)

What the Ovidrel label says, with the Novarel and Pregnyl hCG labels. Your clinic sets the trigger time.

Progesterone in oil

Intramuscular progesterone in sesame oil. IVF luteal-phase support isn’t on its US label.

Other medicines in Jabbit’s library

  • Leuprolide acetate injection. The US label for the 14-day kit lists one indication: palliative treatment of advanced prostatic cancer. Use in IVF isn’t on its US label.[11]
  • Human chorionic gonadotropin (Novarel, Pregnyl). Intramuscular only. Their US labels list ovulation induction in anovulatory infertile women, but not ART or IVF; see the trigger shot page.[8][9]
  • Estradiol valerate (Delestrogen). Intramuscular. Its US label lists menopausal vasomotor symptoms and vulvar and vaginal atrophy, hypoestrogenism due to hypogonadism, castration or primary ovarian failure, and palliation of advanced androgen-dependent prostate cancer. Use in IVF isn’t on its US label.[12]

What Jabbit does, and doesn’t do

Jabbit is a medication diary and calendar. It records the medicines, doses, times and appointments your clinic gives you and reminds you at the times you set. It does not diagnose, treat, calculate doses or timing, or give medical advice. Always follow your clinic’s instructions.

Your records stay on your iPhone, not on our servers, and there is no iCloud sync. To keep a copy, use Export My Data in Settings.

How these pages are written

Each page uses only the medicine’s US prescribing information on DailyMed, with the label’s revision date and the date we checked it. Brand names appear in plain text after the generic name. Where a common IVF use isn’t on a medicine’s US label, the page says so. Our own notes, such as cautions about comparing trials, are labeled as ours. Spotted an error?
