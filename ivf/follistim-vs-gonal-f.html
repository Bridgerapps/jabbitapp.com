Follitropin beta (Follistim AQ) vs follitropin alfa (Gonal-f): what the US labels say, side by side

Follitropin beta (sold as Follistim AQ Cartridge) and follitropin alfa (sold as Gonal-f and Gonal-f RFF Redi-ject) are both recombinant human follicle stimulating hormone (FSH) made in Chinese hamster ovary cell lines, according to their US labels.[1][2][3] This page sets the labels’ facts side by side. It doesn’t recommend one over the other; your clinic chooses the medicine.

What the Follistim label says about switching

The Follistim AQ Cartridge label says the cartridge with the pen injector delivers on average an 18% higher amount of follitropin beta than reconstituted Follistim given with a conventional syringe, and that switching brands, types or methods of administration may require a dose adjustment.[1] Only your clinic can tell you whether your dose changes. Jabbit doesn’t convert doses between products.

Reading the table (our notes)

  • Different trials. Each label reports its own trials in different patients and settings, so the side-effect rates can’t be compared to rank the medicines. These notes are ours, not label statements.
  • Warnings in common. All three labels warn about ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome, serious pulmonary and thromboembolic events, ovarian torsion, multi-fetal gestation, ectopic pregnancy and spontaneous abortion, and say not to double a missed dose.[1][2][3]
  • No recommendation. This page doesn’t rank them. Your clinic chooses the medicine and gives you the dose and the times.

FAQ

Is Follistim the same as Gonal-f?
No. Both are recombinant FSH, but Follistim AQ is follitropin beta and Gonal-f is follitropin alfa, each with its own US label, supply, storage and dosing section.
Which is better, Follistim or Gonal-f?
This page doesn’t rank them. Each label reports its own trials, so their side-effect rates can’t be compared directly. Your clinic chooses the medicine.
Can I switch between Follistim and Gonal-f?
The Follistim AQ Cartridge label says switching brands, types or methods of administration may require a dose adjustment. Only your clinic can decide whether and how to switch.
Do Follistim and Gonal-f need to be refrigerated?
Both labels allow refrigerated storage, with limited room-temperature storage before first use: up to 25°C for 3 months for Follistim AQ, and 20–25°C for up to three months for the Gonal-f RFF Redi-ject pen. Gonal-f multi-dose vials may be kept refrigerated or at room temperature. See the table for after first use.
