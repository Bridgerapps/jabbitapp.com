What to log (the minimum that actually helps)

You don’t need a spreadsheet hobby. You need enough detail to answer: “what changed?” and “what happened after that?”

Core fields

  • Date & time (when you took it)
  • Compound (name / label)
  • Amount (exact amount you took, as instructed or as chosen)
  • Route (e.g., injection / oral) and site (if injected)
  • Symptoms (what you felt, severity, and when it started)
  • Notes (sleep, meals, travel, training, stress — anything that could confound your read)

Optional fields (high value when troubleshooting)

  • Reconstitution details (date mixed + concentration) when applicable.
  • Vial/lot identifiers (for consistency and safety recall checks).
  • Storage events (power outage, travel, temperature issues).

“Am I tracking the right thing?”

If your goal is clarity, track outcomes that map to real decisions:

  • Symptoms (start time, severity, duration)
  • Sleep and energy
  • Training / activity
  • Food timing and anything unusual
