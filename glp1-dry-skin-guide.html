GLP-1 Dry Skin Guide: Causes, Remedies & Prevention

Dry skin is one of the most common side effects of GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy, and Zepbound. While often manageable, neglecting dry skin can lead to discomfort and complications. This guide covers why GLP-1s cause dry skin and proven strategies to find relief.

Why GLP-1 Medications Cause Dry Skin

GLP-1 receptor agonists affect your body in several ways that can lead to dry, itchy skin:

  1. Reduced Oil Production: GLP-1s can decrease sebum (skin oil) production, leaving skin dry and prone to flaking.
  2. Dehydration: These medications cause increased urination and fluid loss, which can dehydrate your skin from the inside out.
  3. Blood Sugar Fluctuations: Improved blood sugar control can affect skin hydration levels, especially in the first few weeks.
  4. Nutrient Absorption Changes: Some users report reduced absorption of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) that are essential for skin health.
  5. GI Side Effects: Nausea and vomiting can lead to electrolyte imbalances that affect skin elasticity and moisture.

10 Proven Remedies for GLP-1 Dry Skin

1. Increase Water Intake

Aim for 64-80 oz of water daily. Add electrolytes if you're experiencing significant fluid loss from urination.

2. Switch to a Gentle, Hydrating Cleanser

Avoid harsh soaps that strip natural oils. Look for:

  • Ceramide-based cleansers
  • Cream or milk cleansers
  • Fragrance-free formulas

3. Apply Moisturizer Immediately After Showering

Pat skin dry and apply moisturizer within 3 minutes of bathing to lock in hydration.

4. Use a Thick, Occlusive Moisturizer

For severely dry skin, use products with:

  • Petrolatum (Vaseline)
  • Ceramides
  • Hyaluronic acid
  • Squalane
  • Shea butter

5. Add Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Supplement with fish oil (2-3g EPA/DHA daily) or eat fatty fish 2-3x per week to support skin cell membranes.

6. Take a Vitamin D Supplement

Many GLP-1 users are deficient in vitamin D. Aim for 2000-4000 IU daily, especially in winter months.

7. Use a Humidifier

Run a humidifier in your bedroom, especially during winter or in dry climates. Target 30-50% humidity.

8. Apply Colloidal Oatmeal Treatments

Colloidal oatmeal baths can soothe itchy, irritated skin. Add 1 cup to warm bathwater.

9. Choose Lukewarm Water

Hot showers strip skin oils. Keep water warm, not hot, and limit showers to 10 minutes or less.

10. See a Dermatologist if Needed

If dry skin persists or worsens, consult a dermatologist. They may recommend:

  • Prescription-strength moisturizers
  • Topical steroids for severe inflammation
  • Blood tests for nutritional deficiencies

When Does Dry Skin Typically Start?

Dry skin usually appears within the first 2-4 weeks of starting a GLP-1 medication, often coinciding with the titration period. For most users, symptoms improve as the body adjusts, typically by weeks 8-12.

FAQ: GLP-1 Dry Skin

Will dry skin go away on its own?

For some users, dry skin improves within 8-12 weeks as the body adapts. However, many continue to experience dryness throughout treatment and benefit from ongoing skincare.

Can I use retinol while on GLP-1 medications?

Yes, but be cautious. Retinol can increase skin sensitivity and dryness. Start with lower concentrations and use sparingly. Always use sunscreen.

Does dry skin mean the medication isn't working?

No. Dry skin is a common side effect unrelated to medication efficacy. Many users experience excellent weight loss results alongside dry skin.

Are there specific moisturizers you recommend?

Popular options include CeraVe Moisturizing Cream, Vanicream Moisturizing Skin Cream, La Roche-Posay Lipikar Balm, and Eucerin Advanced Repair.

Key Takeaways

  • Dry skin affects a significant portion of GLP-1 users
  • Main causes: dehydration, reduced oil production, nutrient absorption changes
  • Remedies focus on hydration (internal and topical)
  • Most cases improve with consistent skincare routine
  • Consult a dermatologist if over-the-counter remedies aren't helping
