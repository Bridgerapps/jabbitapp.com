GLP-1 and Alcohol

Can you drink on Mounjaro, Ozempic, Zepbound?

The Short Answer

It's complicated. Alcohol isn't strictly forbidden but requires caution.

Why Be Careful

  • Blood sugar swings — Alcohol affects glucose
  • Increased nausea — Both cause stomach issues
  • Dehydration — Both cause fluid loss
  • Liver stress — Medication + alcohol = liver works harder

If You Do Drink

Limit: 1-2 drinks max
  • Never drink on empty stomach
  • Stay hydrated — water between drinks
  • Avoid sugary mixed drinks
  • Monitor blood sugar if diabetic
