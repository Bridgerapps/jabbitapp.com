GLP-1 Injection Sites

Where to inject Mounjaro, Ozempic, Zepbound

3 Approved Injection Sites

1. Abdomen (Stomach)

Most common. At least 2 inches from belly button. Inject into fatty tissue.

2. Thigh

Good alternative. Front or outer thigh. Pinch fat when injecting.

3. Upper Arm

Harder to reach. Back of upper arm. Have someone else inject if possible.

Tips

  • Rotate sites — Don't inject same spot each time
  • Avoid scars — Don't inject into scar tissue
  • Room temperature — Let pen warm up 15 min
  • Pinch fat — Helps with injection
