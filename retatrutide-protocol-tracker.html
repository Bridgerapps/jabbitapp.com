What to track (high-signal fields)

Keep it boring and consistent. You’re building a timeline you can trust.

  • Injection timestamp (date + time)
  • Dose as prescribed (write it exactly as given; don’t “normalize”)
  • Injection site (rotation notes)
  • Missed / late dose (what happened + why)
  • Side effects timeline (symptom + time since dose)
  • Context (travel, illness, sleep disruption, big diet changes)
