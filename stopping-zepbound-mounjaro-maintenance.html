Stopping Zepbound or Mounjaro: Maintenance Tracker

For tirzepatide users, pauses, switches, access issues, or stopping can change the weight and appetite timeline. Tracking keeps the signal visible.

What SURMOUNT-4 suggests

In SURMOUNT-4, participants who continued tirzepatide maintained or extended weight loss, while those switched to placebo regained weight. That supports a practical tracking point: stopping or pausing can change trajectory, and the change may show up through appetite and weekly trend before it feels obvious.
