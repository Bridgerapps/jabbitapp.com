   - Start with a hub-style tracker page, then add brand/drug modifiers only if traffic or Search Console evidence supports them.

   - Alcohol and gambling are plausible clusters because GLP-1 reward/craving interest is expanding, but pages must stay harm-reduction and non-treatment.
   - Best-fit pages are logs: alcohol craving tracker, gambling urge tracker, craving timeline, trigger notes, sleep/stress/alcohol context.

## What to deprioritize

- Generic obesity-drug news with no strong tracker path.
- Generic addiction, gambling, alcohol, or longevity advice pages that do not route into a concrete private tracking workflow.
- Prescriptive content about treating addiction, tapering/stopping GLP-1s, dosing protocols, or life-extension interventions.
- Broad peptide science pages unless they clearly support logging, protocols, symptom tracking, or routine management.
- More generic GLP-1 explainers when a brand-specific or task-specific workflow page would capture cleaner intent.
- Candidate queues that ignore the live site and accidentally re-propose already-published pages like `peptide-morning-vs-night-injection-log.html`, `peptide-appetite-energy-log.html`, or `peptide-recovery-sleep-log.html`.
