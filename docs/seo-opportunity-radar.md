# SEO Opportunity Radar (Living)

This is a lightweight, high-signal view of what we should ship next for qualified SEO page volume. It intentionally focuses on *search intent → useful page → App Store click* (not “publish more words”).

## Current thesis (not GLP-1-only)

- Build **tracking utilities** for obesity medications and peptide/protocol logging: missed dose, dose timing, side-effect timelines, dose logs, protocol logs, and drug-specific trackers.
- Create a small set of **hub pages** that route to the right “do the thing” page fast (and convert with clear CTAs).
- Measure by **landing-page mix**, **hub/page → App Store click rate**, and **clicks → installs lagged**. Treat “organic-only” reporting as optional until attribution is trustworthy.

## What’s working (observed)

- A few pages concentrate engagement (example: GLP-1 stress/HRV guides + utilities). This implies **hubs and practical guides beat news posts** for durable intent.
- App Store clicks are happening, but appear concentrated on the homepage; content pages should improve conversion and internal routing.

## Now (ship pages in winning clusters)

Prioritize one-page actions that are templateable and expandable into clusters:

- Retatrutide tracker intent (hub + subpages)
- Peptide logging / protocol intent (dose log + protocol + reconstitution + side-effect log)
- Side-effect timelines (“what to track + when”)
- Missed dose / dose timing (brand + generic where it earns)
- Drug-specific tracker parity (missing sister pages under high-coverage drugs)
- Hubs that route quickly (GLP-1 hub, peptide hub, retatrutide hub)

## Next (after we have conversion signal)

- Consolidate/redirect thin overlaps into canonical hubs.
- Add structured FAQ blocks where it captures modifier queries (timeline, side effects, missed dose, timing).
- Tighten internal linking so every cluster page links to at least one tracker page and at least one hub.

## Notes / guardrails

- Pages must be **useful without medical advice**: logging and organization framing, not prescribing.
- Every page should include:
  - at least one App Store CTA
  - at least one internal link to a tracker/app page
  - `/assets/site.js`
