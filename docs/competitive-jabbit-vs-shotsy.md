Updated: 2026-02-28
Purpose: give Jabby a factual, repeatable positioning doc for Reddit and other channels.

## TL;DR positioning

- **Shotsy**: strong GLP-1-focused tracker with polished charts and broad user adoption.
- **Jabbit**: broader peptide + protocol operations tool (vials, reconstitution, serum curves, 65+ compounds) with privacy-first architecture and lower annual entry pricing (as currently listed).

## Practical differentiation we can actually use

1) **Scope**
- Shotsy messaging is primarily GLP-1 journey tracking.
- Jabbit can be framed as broader protocol tracking (GLP-1 + wider peptide workflows).

2) **Workflow depth**
- Jabbit has explicit vial/reconstitution + site rotation + serum visualization combo.
- This is useful for users managing more complex routines than weekly shot logging.

3) **Price framing (careful)**
- Safe claim: "Annual App Store pricing has been lower on Jabbit than Shotsy as of Feb 2026 listings."
- Avoid absolute evergreen claims like "always cheaper".

4) **Privacy framing**
- Both products use privacy-first language around iCloud/private storage.
- Don’t claim exclusive privacy advantage.

## Claim safety guide (for comments/posts)

### Green (safe)
- "I switched because Jabbit had vial + reconstitution tracking, which I needed."
- "Jabbit supports broader peptide tracking, not just GLP-1 logging."
- "As listed right now, Jabbit annual pricing is lower than Shotsy annual pricing."

### Yellow (use sparingly, add context)
- "More features" → say which features.
- "Better" → tie to personal workflow ("better for me").

### Red (avoid)
- "Jabbit is always cheaper" (not guaranteed over time/region).
- "Shotsy has fewer features" without specifying domain/context.
- Any fake personal experience or fabricated migration story.
