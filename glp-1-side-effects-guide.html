What to log on a side-effect timeline

  • Dose context: injection date, time, and whether it was a starter week, increase week, restart, or normal repeat.
  • Symptom window: 0 to 24 hours, 1 to 3 days, and the rest of the week.
  • Pattern modifiers: sleep debt, meals, hydration, alcohol, travel, exercise load, stress, or illness.
  • Schedule drift: whether the shot was late, missed, or moved to a different day.
  • Outcome: improved, stable, worse, or unclear compared with the prior week.
