FAQ

Do GLP-1 side effects always follow the same day-by-day pattern?
No. The useful move is not assuming a universal timeline. It is logging your own week clearly enough to see whether symptoms cluster after dose day, after a dose increase, after a late dose, or for unrelated reasons like poor sleep or dehydration.
Should anxiety, HRV, and resting heart rate live on the same timeline as nausea or fatigue?
Usually yes. A combined timeline helps separate digestive symptoms, stress, low intake, poor sleep, and wearable changes instead of forcing you to guess later.
Do missed-dose weeks need their own tracker?
Usually yes. A late or skipped injection can distort the whole week, so it helps to label that week clearly instead of treating it like a normal pattern.
