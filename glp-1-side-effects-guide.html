Simple side-effect timeline template

Keep the log boring and consistent. The goal is not to write more. It is to make the pattern legible.

Window What to log Why it matters
Shot day Exact dose time, dose, injection note, planned time vs actual time if the shot was late Separates a normal week from a timing-shift week before symptoms even start.
0-24 hours Nausea, fullness, headache, wired feeling, resting HR, HRV, sleepiness, or no symptoms Captures the immediate curve instead of relying on memory later.
1-3 days Appetite, constipation, fatigue, anxiety, hydration, food intake, bowel changes, recovery notes Many patterns show up here, especially if the week also had low intake or poor sleep.
3-7 days Whether symptoms faded, repeated, or lined up with another change like travel or alcohol Stops you from treating a random stressful week like a medication pattern.
Weekly review Better, same, or worse than last week plus the biggest confounder Turns the log into a comparison tool instead of a pile of notes.
