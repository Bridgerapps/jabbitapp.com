="description" content Weight loss stalled on your GLP-1 medication? Before asking for a dose increase, work through this checklist. Most plateaus have solvable causes.

🔍 5 Common Plateau Causes

📅 7-Day Troubleshooting Protocol

Day 1-2: Focus on hydration + sleep tracking

Day 3-4: Add fiber supplement, log all meals

Day 5-6: Increase protein, add 10-min walk after meals

Day 7: Review data, identify patterns

⚠️ When to Contact Your Provider:
  • Plateau persists beyond 4 weeks despite protocol
  • Unexplained weight gain (not water)
  • New symptoms appearing

📊 What to Bring to Your Next Appointment

Providers appreciate data. Before your check-in, prepare:

  • Weight trend (last 8 weeks)
  • Dose history + dates
  • Symptom log (nausea, energy, sleep quality)
  • Key questions written down
