How to read the pattern without overreacting

  • Look for repeatable timing. A similar Day 1 or Day 2 pattern across several weeks is more useful than one bad night.
  • Mark dose changes clearly. Escalation, pause, and restart weeks create noisier signals and need better notes.
  • Tag confounders aggressively. Sleep debt, under-eating, dehydration, illness, and stimulant use can dominate HRV and anxiety changes.
  • Separate schedule drift from symptom drift. If the week was late, skipped, or moved, use the retatrutide missed-dose tracker or changing-injection-day log so the timeline stays interpretable.
