Hims & Hers drops compounded “Wegovy pill” plan after FDA warning

On March 9, 2026, the Los Angeles Times reported that Hims & Hers abandoned its plan for a cheaper compounded alternative to Wegovy after an FDA warning.[1]

The FDA statement behind it

A month earlier, FDA said it intended to restrict GLP-1 active ingredients for non-approved compounded drugs mass-marketed as similar alternatives to approved drugs, and named Hims & Hers specifically.[2]

What companies cannot claim

FDA said promotional materials cannot call non-approved compounded products generic versions of, or the same as, approved drugs, and cannot claim they are clinically proven to produce results.[2] Its March 2026 telehealth warning letters focused on the same issues.[3]
