GLP-1 Diet Guide

What to eat on Ozempic, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Wegovy to maximize results.

🥗 What to Eat

✅ Protein - chicken, fish, eggs, tofu
✅ Vegetables - leafy greens, broccoli, peppers
✅ Healthy fats - avocado, olive oil, nuts
✅ High-fiber - oats, beans, berries
✅ Lean meats - turkey, fish, lean beef
✅ Greek yogurt - high protein, low sugar

🚫 What to Avoid

❌ Fried foods - trigger nausea
❌ High sugar - increases side effects
❌ Processed foods - hard to digest
❌ Alcohol - worsens side effects
❌ Carbonated drinks - bloating
❌ Large portions - start small

🍽️ Sample Day of Eating

Breakfast: Greek yogurt + berries + nuts

Lunch: Grilled chicken + mixed greens + olive oil

Snack: Apple slices + almond butter

Dinner: Baked salmon + roasted vegetables

💡 Tips for Success

  1. Eat smaller meals - Your stomach empties slower on GLP-1s
  2. Focus on protein - Prevents muscle loss during weight loss
  3. Stay hydrated - Drink 64+ oz water daily
  4. Eat slowly - Gives your body time to feel full
  5. Don't skip meals - Can cause nausea
