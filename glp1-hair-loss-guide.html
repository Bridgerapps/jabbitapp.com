GLP-1 Hair Loss Guide

Causes, Prevention & Recovery — What Actually Works

You're not imagining it. Many people on GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, or Tirzepatide notice increased hair shedding—sometimes starting 2-4 months after beginning treatment. This guide explains why it happens and what you can do about it.

Good news: This type of hair loss is usually temporary (telogen effluvium), and hair typically regrows once your body adjusts or you reach a stable weight.

Why GLP-1 Medications Cause Hair Loss

Hair loss on GLP-1 drugs isn't caused by the medication itself attacking your hair follicles. Instead, it's usually telogen effluvium—a temporary shedding response triggered by physical stress on your body.

Key Causes:

  • Rapid weight loss — Your body thinks it's starving and shifts hair follicles into resting mode
  • Caloric restriction — Eating significantly less can mean less protein, iron, zinc, and biotin
  • Nutrient deficiencies — Even with healthy eating, rapid weight loss can outpace nutrient absorption
  • Hormonal shifts — GLP-1s affect hormones that influence hair growth cycles
  • Stress on the body — Significant metabolic changes are stressful, even when positive
Important: Hair loss typically starts 2-4 months after beginning GLP-1 treatment or after significant weight loss, not immediately.

How to Prevent Hair Loss on GLP-1 Medications

1. Prioritize Protein

Aim for 0.7-1 gram of protein per pound of body weight daily. Hair is made of keratin, a protein. Low protein intake signals your body to conserve resources by reducing hair growth.

2. Don't Skip Meals

GLP-1 medications reduce appetite, but your hair (and the rest of your body) still needs nutrients. Focus on nutrient-dense foods rather than drastically reducing calories.

3. Supplement Strategically

  • Biotin (vitamin B7) — 2,500-5,000 mcg daily
  • Vitamin D — 2,000-5,000 IU daily (most people are deficient)
  • Iron — Get levels tested; supplement if deficient
  • Zinc — 15-30 mg daily
  • Collagen peptides — 10-20 grams daily (mix in coffee, smoothies)
Pro tip: Consider a comprehensive hair/skin/nails vitamin like Nutrafol, Viviscal, or a quality biotin complex.

4. Be Gentle with Your Hair

  • Use a wide-tooth comb on wet hair
  • Avoid tight hairstyles (ponytails, braids)
  • Limit heat styling
  • Use sulfate-free, gentle shampoos
  • Pat hair dry—don't rub

What If Hair Loss Has Already Started?

Don't panic. Here's what works for recovery:

1. Continue Your GLP-1 Medication

Stopping suddenly can cause more stress on your body. If hair loss is concerning you, talk to your provider about maintaining your current dose.

2. Minoxidil (Rogaine)

Topical minoxidil can help shorten the recovery time. It's available over-the-counter in 2% and 5% solutions. Note: You must continue using it to maintain results.

3. Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT)

Devices like laser caps, helmets, and combs can stimulate hair follicles. FDA-cleared options include Capillus and iRestore.

4. PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Injections

For more significant hair loss, some people opt for PRP treatments. A doctor draws your blood, processes it, and injects the plasma into your scalp.

5. Time & Patience

Most telogen effluvium resolves on its own within 3-6 months once the trigger (rapid weight loss) stabilizes. Hair regrowth is slower—expect visible improvement in 6-12 months.

When to See a Doctor

  • Hair loss is severe (handfuls of hair per day)
  • It doesn't stop after 6 months
  • You notice bald patches
  • You have other symptoms (fatigue, brittle nails, skin changes)

A dermatologist can rule out other causes like thyroid issues, autoimmune conditions, or androgenetic alopecia.

FAQ

Will my hair grow back?

Yes. In most cases, hair lost to telogen effluvium grows back. It's a temporary condition triggered by stress on your body.

How long does GLP-1 hair loss last?

Active shedding typically lasts 2-6 months. Full regrowth can take 6-12 months.

Does dose matter?

Rapid dose increases may increase stress on your body. If you're concerned about hair loss, ask your provider about a slower titration schedule.

Do all GLP-1 medications cause hair loss?

Hair loss has been reported with Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, and compound Tirzepatide. It's not specific to any brand.

Is hair loss a sign the medication isn't working?

No. Hair loss is actually common in successful weight loss—it's a sign your body is responding to the medication.
