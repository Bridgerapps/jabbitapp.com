## Common Challenges & Solutions ### "I'm not hungry enough to eat protein"
Solution: Start your day with protein. A high-protein breakfast (eggs, Greek yogurt, protein shake) sets the tone. GLP-1 users report that front-loading protein in the morning works best.
### "Protein shakes make me nauseous"
Solution: Try whey protein isolate, which digests faster. Start with half a scoop. Some users do better with plant-based proteins. Experiment to find what works.
### "I can't hit my protein goals"
Solution: Add protein-rich snacks: string cheese, cottage cheese, jerky, roasted chickpeas. Use protein pancakes or muffins as treats that actually help you.
