Trigger shot calendar: add your clinic’s exact time to your calendar

Type the date and exact time your clinic gave you for your trigger shot. This page makes a calendar file (.ics) with an alarm at that exact time, plus any earlier alarms you choose. Open the file to add the event to your calendar app.

Everything stays in your browser. What you type isn’t sent to Jabbit or anyone else, isn’t saved, and never goes into the page address. Visit counts for this site, described in our privacy policy, never include anything you type here.

This tool doesn’t suggest a time, a lead time or a reminder. It has no default alarms other than the exact time. Use the exact time your clinic gave you, and always follow your clinic’s instructions.

If your browser shows a plain text box, type it as YYYY-MM-DD.
If your browser shows a plain text box, type it as 24-hour HH:MM.
Alarms

An alarm at the exact time is always included. You can add up to five earlier alarms; there are none unless you add them.

    How the file works

    • The event starts at the date and time you typed, read in this device’s time zone, which the preview shows. If you’ll be in a different time zone, check the event in your calendar.
    • Each alarm is part of the event. Some calendar apps replace imported alarms with their own default alerts, so open the event after adding it and check its alerts.
    • The file is named trigger-shot.ics and isn’t uploaded anywhere. Delete it from your downloads when you no longer need it.
