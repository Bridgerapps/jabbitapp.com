What most people actually need: a system

If your injection day moves around because of travel, busy weeks, or just forgetting, a reminder + quick log is usually enough to keep you consistent.

A reminder setup that works

Practical tip: pick a day/time you’re usually home and set a backup reminder the next day.
  • Primary reminder: your injection day/time
  • Backup reminder: 12–24 hours later
  • Log prompt: immediately after injecting

What to log (keep it lightweight)

  • Date & time
  • Medication (e.g., Ozempic / Wegovy)
  • Dose (exactly as prescribed)
  • Injection site (for rotation habits)
  • One note (sleep, nausea, appetite, travel, etc.)
