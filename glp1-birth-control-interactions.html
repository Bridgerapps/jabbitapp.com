GLP-1 and Birth Control

Interactions between GLP-1 medications and contraceptives

The Short Answer

GLP-1 doesn't directly affect birth control. But there's a catch...

The Absorption Issue

GLP-1 slows stomach emptying. This may affect absorption of oral contraceptives.

If you experience significant nausea/vomiting, effectiveness may be reduced.

Recommendations

  • Consider backup — Use condoms during first month
  • Non-oral options — IUD, implant, patch may be safer
  • Talk to doctor — Discuss your specific situation
  • If vomiting — Use backup protection
