What is Retatrutide?

Retatrutide (formerly known as "Triple G") is an experimental medication from Eli Lilly that targets three hormones: GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon. This triple mechanism leads to unprecedented weight loss results.

Retatrutide Weight Loss Results

  • 24% body weight loss at 48 weeks (highest dose)
  • Significant reduction in liver fat
  • Improved insulin sensitivity
  • Currently in Phase 3 trials

When Will Retatrutide Be Available?

Retatrutide is currently in Phase 3 clinical trials. Expected FDA approval: 2026-2027. It will likely be marketed under a brand name similar to Mounjaro/Zepbound.
