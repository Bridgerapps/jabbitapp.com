What happened (high level)

In March 2026, the FDA announced approval of a higher-dose semaglutide product associated with Wegovy coverage, with reporting that referenced a 7.2 mg dose and the FDA’s National Priority Voucher pilot.

Why this matters for you: Dose-strength changes (or new strengths coming to market) can create confusion at the pharmacy and at home—especially if you have older boxes, new boxes, and insurer paperwork floating around.

If your Wegovy strength or dose changed, track this (to avoid mix-ups)

Your goal isn’t perfect journaling. It’s having one reliable record you can trust—especially when you’re asked “when did you start this strength?”

Minimum viable tracking (takes ~30 seconds/week)

  • Injection date & time (timestamp)
  • Product/strength as written on the box (take a quick note like “Wegovy — ___ mg”)
  • Where you stored it (fridge location is surprisingly helpful in busy homes)
  • Refill date (or “requested refill” date)
  • One logistics note (e.g., “pharmacy partial fill,” “prior auth pending,” “travel week”)

The three common failure modes (and what to do instead)

  • “Did I inject already?” → log immediately after injecting (one trusted log beats memory).
  • “Which box/strength am I on?” → record the exact strength + the start date of that strength.
  • Refill chaos (paperwork, delays, partials) → track events (requested, filled, picked up) so you can explain it quickly.

A 2‑reminder setup that actually works for weekly meds

  1. Primary reminder: same day/time every week (your “anchor”).
  2. Backup check-in: next morning, quick prompt: “Is it logged?”
  3. Rule: you don’t mark it done until it’s logged.

What should I track when my dose or pen strength changes?
Track the exact product name/strength on the box, the date you started it, your weekly injection timestamps, and refill/insurance notes.
What’s the simplest reminder setup for a weekly injection?
Use one recurring reminder on the same day/time each week, plus an optional next-morning “confirm it’s logged” reminder.
