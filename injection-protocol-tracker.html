What “protocol tracking” means

Many people aren’t tracking just a single weekly injection — they’re tracking a routine: dose changes over time, a supply cycle, a rotation pattern, and progress. Jabbit is built to make that calm and consistent.

Built for GLP‑1 (and flexible when your routine is more complex)

  • Reminders that match your schedule
  • Dose history with notes you can actually find later
  • Site rotation notes to keep patterns straight
  • Vial-aware inventory (so you know what you have, not just what you took)
  • Progress tracking (photos + measurements) in one timeline
