What “protocol tracking” means (log-first, not advice)

If you’re tracking an investigational compound, the main challenge is consistency: capturing what happened, when it happened, and what you noticed. Jabbit is a private log + reminder system for that workflow.

What you can track for retatrutide / peptides

  • Dose history: date/time, amount, lot/vial notes (optional)
  • Protocol notes: what changed, why, and what you observed
  • Side-effect timeline: attach notes to specific dates (patterns over time)
  • Missed dose / timing: log what happened so you can reference it later
  • Site rotation: simple tracking so you don’t lose the pattern
