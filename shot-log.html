A simple shot log for your iPhone

If you take regular shots, a paper list or a notes app works until it doesn’t. Jabbit is a shot log built for injections: date, time, site, vial and your own notes, in one place.

Jabbit is an injection log for iPhone: injections, vials and sites in one place.

Download on the App Store

What you can record

  • The date and time of each shot
  • The site you used
  • The vial it came from
  • Notes in your own words

Reminders at the times you set

Choose your own reminder times. Jabbit reminds you; it doesn’t set or change a schedule.

Your history at a glance

Scroll back through past entries whenever you need them. Jabbit shows what you entered and doesn’t interpret it.

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants a written record of their prescribed injections. Questions about your medicine belong with your prescriber.
