What to log (minimum viable timeline)

This page is intentionally non-medical. It does not tell you what dose to take or when to take it. It helps you capture the facts so you can reason from a timeline instead of memory.

Core fields

  • Scheduled dose day/time so you can see the original plan
  • Actual injection day/time if you took it later
  • Medication name and dose exactly as prescribed
  • Reason for the miss or delay such as travel, forgot, refill issue, or side effects
  • Symptoms + context including appetite, nausea, constipation, sleep, meals, and stress
  • Next scheduled dose so your routine does not get fuzzy afterward

FAQ

Does this page tell me what to do after a missed dose?
No. This is a tracking template that helps you log what happened and build a clean timeline. For dose timing decisions, follow your prescriber and the medication’s labeling.
What is the minimum I should log?
Log the scheduled dose time, the actual injection time if it changed, and one sentence on why the timing shifted. That alone makes future review much easier.
Can I track symptoms too?
Yes. Add symptoms and context such as sleep, meals, stress, and travel. The goal is to connect timing changes with what you noticed next.
Is this only for Mounjaro?
No. The same structure works for GLP‑1 and tirzepatide tracking more broadly, but this page is written for Mounjaro-specific search intent.

Quick checklist

  • Write down the scheduled time first.
  • Then log the actual injection time, if different.
  • Add one reason and one context note.
  • Set the next reminder immediately.
