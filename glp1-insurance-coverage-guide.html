Does Your Insurance Cover Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, or Zepbound?

Does Insurance Cover GLP-1 Medications?

Insurance coverage for GLP-1 medications (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) varies dramatically by plan. Here's what you need to know:

Key Stat: Approximately 50% of commercial insurance plans now cover at least one GLP-1 medication for weight loss, up from 25% in 2023. Medicare Part D still has limited coverage.

Coverage by Insurance Type

Medicare Part D

  • Ozempic, Rybelsus, Victoza: Covered for type 2 diabetes
  • Wegovy, Zepbound: Generally NOT covered for weight loss alone
  • Exception: Some Medicare Advantage plans offer coverage

Commercial Insurance (BCBS, Aetna, United, Cigna)

  • Most cover Ozempic for diabetes (step therapy may apply)
  • Wegovy coverage: Growing, but often requires prior authorization
  • Zepbound: Increasingly covered with BMI requirements
  • Prior authorization: Almost always required

Medicaid

  • Coverage varies significantly by state
  • Some states (CA, NY, MA) have expanded coverage
  • Prior authorization common

How to Get Insurance to Cover Your GLP-1

Step 1: Check Your Specific Plan

Call the number on your insurance card and ask:

  • "Is [medication name] covered for weight loss?"
  • "What are the prior authorization requirements?"
  • "What is the step therapy requirement?"
  • "What is my copay with coupon/without coupon?"

Step 2: Meet the Criteria

Most plans require ONE of these:

  • BMI ≥ 30 (obesity)
  • BMI ≥ 27 with at least one weight-related comorbidity (hypertension, type 2 diabetes, sleep apnea, etc.)
  • Documented failed attempts at diet and exercise

Step 3: Prior Authorization

Your doctor will need to submit a prior authorization (PA) including:

  • Current BMI and weight
  • Weight-related comorbidities
  • Previous weight loss attempts (diet, exercise, medications)
  • Why other treatments failed

Insurance Appeal Tips

If denied, you have the right to appeal. Here's how:

  1. Request the denial in writing - Know exactly why you were denied
  2. Gather supporting documentation - Medical records, diet logs, previous treatments
  3. Write a letter of medical necessity - Your doctor can help with this
  4. File the appeal within 30 days of denial
  5. Request a peer-to-peer review - Your doctor talks directly to the insurance medical director
Success Tip: Appeals are successful approximately 40% of the time when proper documentation is provided.

Cost-Saving Strategies

Manufacturer Coupons

  • Ozempic: Novo Nordisk coupon - $25/month for commercially insured
  • Wegovy: Novo Nordisk coupon - $25/month for commercially insured
  • Mounjaro/Zepbound: Eli Lilly coupon - $25/month for commercially insured

Patient Assistance Programs

  • Novo Nordisk Patient Assistance Program: Free medication for qualifying patients (income-based)
  • Eli Lilly Patient Assistance Program: Free medication for qualifying patients

Other Options

  • Health Savings Account (HSA): Use pre-tax dollars
  • Flexible Spending Account (FSA): Use pre-tax dollars
  • Compound pharmacies: Often 50-70% cheaper (discuss with your provider)

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Blue Cross Blue Shield cover GLP-1s for weight loss?

Coverage varies by specific BCBS plan. Many do cover Wegovy or Zepbound with prior authorization. Call your plan to verify.

Does UnitedHealthcare cover Ozempic for weight loss?

UnitedHealthcare increasingly covers GLP-1s for weight loss, but prior authorization is required. Coverage may depend on your specific employer plan.

How long does prior authorization take?

Typically 3-7 business days. Some insurers offer expedited review (24-48 hours) if medically necessary.

What if my insurance denies coverage?

File an appeal immediately. Many denials are overturned with additional documentation. You can also ask about manufacturer coupons or patient assistance programs.

Can I use insurance for compound GLP-1s?

Generally no - insurance typically doesn't cover compound medications. However, they can be significantly cheaper out-of-pocket.

What's the cheapest GLP-1 without insurance?

Generic options are limited. Rybelsus (oral semaglutide) can be around $900/month without insurance. Compounded versions can be $200-400/month. Always consult with a healthcare provider.

Does Tricare cover GLP-1 medications?

Tricare covers Ozempic, Rybelsus, and Victoza for type 2 diabetes. Coverage for weight loss (Wegovy, Zepbound) is limited and requires prior authorization.

What BMI is needed for insurance coverage?

Most insurance plans require BMI ≥ 30, or BMI ≥ 27 with at least one weight-related comorbidity (type 2 diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, sleep apnea, etc.).

How do I qualify for Wegovy covered by insurance?

Meet your plan's criteria (typically BMI 30+ or 27+ with comorbidity), get a prescription from your doctor, and have your doctor submit a prior authorization.

Is Zepbound covered by insurance?

Many commercial insurers now cover Zepbound for weight loss with prior authorization. Check with your specific plan for details.
