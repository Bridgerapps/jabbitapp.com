GLP-1 Medications and Fertility: What the Research Shows

If you're considering GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy, or Zepbound and wondering about their impact on fertility and pregnancy, this guide covers what you need to know.

⚠️ Important: Always consult with your healthcare provider before making any changes to your medication regimen, especially if you're planning pregnancy or are currently pregnant.

Can GLP-1 Medications Affect Fertility?

Research suggests that GLP-1 medications may actually improve fertility in some individuals, particularly those with obesity or polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS). Here's why:

  • Weight loss improves hormone balance – Losing 5-10% of body weight can significantly improve ovarian function
  • Reduced inflammation – GLP-1 medications have anti-inflammatory effects that may benefit reproductive health
  • Improved insulin sensitivity – Better insulin regulation can support healthy ovulation
📊 Research Note: A 2023 study published in Obesity found that women with PCOS who used GLP-1 medications showed improved menstrual regularity and ovulation rates.

What About Pregnancy and GLP-1 Medications?

The current recommendation is to stop GLP-1 medications before planning pregnancy. Here's what to consider:

  • Discontinuation timeline: Most guidelines recommend stopping GLP-1s at least 2-3 months before trying to conceive
  • Animal studies: Some GLP-1 medications showed effects on fetal development in animal studies
  • Human data is limited – There aren't large-scale human trials on GLP-1 use during pregnancy

If You're Currently on GLP-1s and Planning Pregnancy

  1. Talk to your doctor – Discuss a transition plan
  2. Focus on sustainable habits – Use this time to build diet and exercise routines
  3. Consider alternative medications – Some providers may recommend other options during pregnancy
  4. Monitor your health – Regular check-ups are essential

GLP-1 Medications and Breastfeeding

Data on GLP-1 medications during breastfeeding is limited. Most providers recommend avoiding these medications while breastfeeding due to unknown effects on breast milk.

Key Takeaways

  • GLP-1s may improve fertility in people with obesity or PCOS
  • Stop GLP-1 medications 2-3 months before planning pregnancy
  • Always consult your healthcare provider
  • Focus on sustainable lifestyle changes during this time

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get pregnant while on Ozempic?

While some women have become pregnant while on GLP-1 medications, the recommendation is to stop these drugs before trying to conceive. Discuss your plans with your healthcare provider.

How long after stopping GLP-1 can I try to conceive?

Most guidelines recommend waiting 2-3 months after stopping GLP-1 medications before attempting pregnancy.

Will I regain weight if I stop GLP-1 for pregnancy?

Some weight regain is common, but focusing on sustainable habits during this time can help maintain progress. Many people find they keep some benefits even after stopping.

Does Mounjaro affect pregnancy?

Mounjaro (tirzepatide) is not recommended during pregnancy. If you become pregnant while taking Mounjaro, contact your healthcare provider immediately.
