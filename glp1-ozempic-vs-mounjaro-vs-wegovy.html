Effectiveness: Which Works Best?

Clinical trials show significant differences in weight loss effectiveness:

  • Mounjaro: Up to 22% body weight loss in trials (SURPASS studies)
  • Wegovy: Approximately 15% body weight loss (STEP trials)
  • Ozempic: Approximately 15% body weight loss (SUSTAIN trials)

Mounjaro (tirzepatide) shows superior effectiveness because it targets both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, while Ozempic and Wegovy only target GLP-1.

Serious Risks

  • Pancreatitis
  • Gallbladder issues
  • Kidney problems
  • Thyroid tumors (rare)
  • Hypoglycemia

Cost Comparison

Without insurance, these medications are expensive:

  • Ozempic: ~$900-1000/month
  • Mounjaro: ~$1000-1200/month
  • Wegovy: ~$1300-1500/month

Manufacturer coupons and insurance coverage can significantly reduce costs.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Mounjaro if: You want maximum weight loss results and your doctor approves it. Studies show it may be most effective.

Choose Wegovy if: You need a medication specifically FDA-approved for weight loss (not just diabetes) and can afford it.

Choose Ozempic if: You have type 2 diabetes and want weight loss as a benefit. Often easier to get insurance coverage.

Insurance Coverage

Coverage varies significantly:

  • Ozempic: Best coverage for diabetes patients
  • Wegovy: Requires prior authorization for weight loss
  • Mounjaro: Coverage expanding but may need appeal

Check with your insurance provider about specific coverage requirements and prior authorization.

Making Your Decision

Consult with your healthcare provider to determine which medication is right for your specific situation. Factors include:

  • Your weight loss goals
  • Current health conditions
  • Insurance coverage
  • Potential side effects
  • Your doctor's recommendation
