Study teardown · Kisspeptin-10 (kisspeptin, metastin, sold online for testosterone, fertility and libido) · 30 years of studies from the 1996 gene discovery to 2026, two drug-company analog programs, FDA and WADA status, registry, FAERS and PubMed counts · Sources checked October 25, 2026

Published October 25, 2026 · Updated October 28, 2026

Kisspeptin-10: does it boost testosterone or restore fertility, and is it safe? A single dose briefly raises the hormones that drive testosterone, but the response fades with repeated or constant exposure, two drug-company trials of an analog for low testosterone were stopped for missing their goals, we found no trials of fertility recovery after TRT, and FDA lists it among compounding ingredients that raise significant safety risks.

Kisspeptin is a natural hormone that sits at the top of the reproductive chain: it tells the brain to release GnRH, which tells the pituitary to release LH and FSH, which tell the testes to make testosterone and sperm.[4][6] In small supervised studies, one dose raised LH and, in young men, testosterone within hours.[7][9][24] But the body tunes out kisspeptin when exposure is frequent or constant, and a potent analog given continuously drove testosterone down into the castration range.[12][20][21] Two drug-company phase 2a trials using an analog to raise low testosterone were terminated after missing their primary goals, and we found no published trial testing kisspeptin to restore fertility after testosterone therapy.[22][27] The honest answer: it is a real hormone with serious research uses, especially in IVF, but there is no good evidence that the injections sold online boost testosterone or fertility over time, and FDA lists kisspeptin-10 among compounding ingredients that raise significant safety risks.[14][29][30]

Why this matters now

Kisspeptin-10 is widely promoted online as an “upstream” way to raise your own testosterone, to restart fertility after TRT or steroids, and as a libido peptide. Three recent developments are relevant. A placebo-controlled study published in August 2026 tested repeated kisspeptin-10 in healthy men and found that the schedule decides whether the response lasts.[26] The first two bulk-ingredient listings for kisspeptin-10 acetate appeared in FDA’s drug directory in January and May 2026, which shows raw powder is being registered for sale, not that any product is approved.[36] And FDA’s interim peptide compounding policy cleared White House review on October 27, 2026; the version HHS made public on October 28 lists six peptides, and kisspeptin-10 is not among them. Kisspeptin-10 has sat in FDA’s category of substances that raise significant safety risks since September 2023.[31][29] For where every peptide stands, see our FDA peptide compounding status tracker.

What kisspeptin is

The KISS1 gene makes a precursor protein that is cut into peptides of different lengths. Kisspeptin-54 is the main form in blood, and kisspeptin-10 is its last ten amino acids, the shortest piece with full activity at the receptor.[2][9][6] Both act mainly in the brain, on the neurons that release GnRH, so kisspeptin works one step above GnRH and two steps above LH and testosterone.[6][5] People born without a working receptor do not go through normal puberty, which is why researchers see it as a master switch for the reproductive system.[3][4] It breaks down quickly: a 2024 review puts the half-life in blood at about 28 minutes for kisspeptin-54 and about 3 minutes for kisspeptin-10, one reason drug companies developed longer-lasting analogs.[7][6] In research, a kisspeptin test can show whether a man’s low hormones come from the brain, separating men with congenital hypogonadotropic hypogonadism from healthy men.[28]

The evidence, rung by rung

  • Short-term hormone rise. In healthy men, a single IV dose or a day-long infusion raised LH and testosterone in studies of 4 to 6 men per group.[7][9] Kisspeptin-10 and kisspeptin-54 behaved similarly, and GnRH itself was more potent than either.[11] More is not always better: the highest single kisspeptin-10 dose tested produced a smaller LH response than a lower one.[9]
  • Age. In one study, a kisspeptin-10 shot raised LH in young, middle-aged and older men, but raised testosterone significantly only in the young group.[24] Another found older men’s brains still responded but their testosterone rise was smaller, which the authors attribute to the testes.[25] That matters for men over 40: the authors note that age-related testosterone decline involves the testes, though brain or pituitary changes may coexist.[25]
  • Women. Kisspeptin-10 did not change LH or FSH in women early in the menstrual cycle, though it did near ovulation.[10][8]
  • Repeated or constant use. Twice-daily injections in women with hypothalamic amenorrhea cut the LH response from 24.0 to 2.5 IU/L over two weeks, while twice-weekly injections faded less.[12][13] Continuous exposure to the analogs TAK-448 and TAK-683 drove testosterone below castration levels in healthy men, which is why they were also studied as prostate cancer treatments. TAK-448 was later renamed MVT-602 and studied in women.[20][21][6][23] In the August 2026 study, a continuous 5-day kisspeptin-10 infusion left LH and FSH no different from placebo, although testosterone stayed raised, while daily 8-hour infusions still worked on day 12.[26]
  • Raising low testosterone. Takeda tested TAK-448 given intermittently in middle-aged and older men with low testosterone, and in men with obesity-related low hormones; both phase 2a trials were terminated for missing their primary endpoints, and no results were posted.[22] We found no completed placebo-controlled trial showing that kisspeptin raises testosterone in men with low levels over weeks or months.[34][37]
  • Fertility after TRT or steroids. This is the most common online claim, and the one with the least direct support. A 2026 review of peptide approaches after TRT concludes that direct evidence for restored sperm production or fertility “remains limited,” and we found no registered trial testing it. Registered trials today mostly study people with diagnosed hormone disorders, such as hypogonadotropic hypogonadism and hypothalamic amenorrhea.[27][34]
  • IVF. The strongest clinical results are in women. One or two kisspeptin-54 shots triggered egg maturation in phase 2 trials, including 60 women at high risk of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome, none of whom developed moderate or severe OHSS.[14][15][16] These trials did not compare kisspeptin head to head with standard triggers.[16]
  • Libido. In brain-scan studies, kisspeptin infusions boosted responses to sexual images, and in two crossover trials of 32 women and 32 men with low sexual desire, single 75-minute IV infusions changed brain activity, with secondary signals such as more penile tumescence in men.[17][18][19] Those were one-time hospital infusions of kisspeptin-54, not repeated home injections of kisspeptin-10.[18][19]

Safety: what is and isn’t known

In the short, supervised studies, kisspeptin was generally well tolerated: the 2010 amenorrhea study and the 2022 low-desire trial in women reported no adverse effects, and adverse events with TAK-683 were similar to placebo.[13][18][21] With TAK-448, 26% of healthy men had mild to moderate adverse events.[20] The total human experience is small: mostly single doses or a few weeks, often given by vein, in studies of a few to a few dozen people.[37][34] Many of the European studies come from one London research group, as the overlapping author lists in our references show.[12][14][19][26]

The biggest known risk is the opposite of the marketing. Frequent or constant exposure blunts the response, and continuous exposure to potent analogs shut testosterone down.[12][20][21][6] Whether that happens with products sold online, at whatever schedule users choose, has not been studied. FDA’s safety concern is about the products themselves: compounded kisspeptin-10 “may pose risk for immunogenicity,” may have problems with impurities, and FDA “lacks sufficient information to know whether the drug would cause harm.”[29] FAERS holds one report naming kisspeptin, but unapproved products bought online are rarely reported, so that says little about safety.[35]

Legal status

No kisspeptin product is approved by FDA, and Drugs@FDA lists none.[35] FDA has placed kisspeptin-10 in 503A category 2, bulk drug substances that raise significant safety risks, since September 29, 2023, and it remains there on the list updated May 14, 2026.[29][30] FDA’s interim peptide policy, made public by HHS on October 28, lists six peptides for limited pharmacy compounding, and kisspeptin-10 is not one of them.[31] Two bulk powders are registered in FDA’s NDC directory, and a listing is not approval.[36] In sport, “kisspeptin and its agonist analogues” are banned at all times for men under WADA’s list, as testosterone-stimulating peptides.[32][33]

What a careful reader should notice

These points are our analysis of the sources cited.

  1. The claim that kisspeptin raises testosterone comes from single doses in small groups of mostly young, healthy men. Nobody has shown a lasting rise in men with low testosterone.[7][9][24][22]
  2. A drug-company attempt to turn kisspeptin into a testosterone-raising drug used an analog, and its two phase 2a trials were stopped for missing their goals.[22]
  3. Timing is everything with this hormone: the same molecule can stimulate or suppress the system depending on how often it is given.[12][26][20]
  4. The impressive IVF and libido results used kisspeptin-54 in hospitals, often by vein, under research protocols. They do not show what home injections of kisspeptin-10 do.[14][15][19]
  5. Kisspeptin-10 did not work in women early in the cycle, and testosterone responses were weaker in older men, so results do not transfer neatly between groups.[10][24][25]
  6. For fertility after TRT, the evidence gap is acknowledged in the research literature itself.[27]

Frequently asked questions

Does kisspeptin-10 raise testosterone?
Briefly, in some people. Single doses raised LH and testosterone in small studies of healthy young men, but testosterone rose less or not significantly in older men, and the response fades with repeated or constant use. No trial has shown a lasting rise in men with low testosterone.[7][9][24][12][22]
Can kisspeptin restore fertility after TRT or steroids?
That has not been tested in a published trial that we could find. A 2026 review says direct evidence that peptides like kisspeptin restore sperm production or fertility after TRT “remains limited.”[27][34]
Is kisspeptin-10 safe?
It looked well tolerated in short, supervised research studies, but long-term safety is unknown. FDA says it lacks the information to know whether compounded kisspeptin-10 would cause harm and flags immune-reaction and impurity risks. Frequent or constant exposure can suppress the reproductive system.[13][18][29][20]
Is kisspeptin FDA approved?
No. There is no approved kisspeptin product in the United States, and FDA lists kisspeptin-10 in its compounding category for substances that raise significant safety risks.[35][29][30]
Does kisspeptin increase libido?
There is an early signal. In two small crossover trials of people with low sexual desire, single hospital IV infusions of kisspeptin-54 changed brain responses to sexual images, with some secondary improvements. Larger and longer trials have not been reported.[18][19]
Is kisspeptin used in IVF?
Only in research so far. Phase 2 trials used kisspeptin-54 to trigger egg maturation, including in women at high risk of ovarian hyperstimulation, with encouraging results, but it is not an approved IVF drug.[14][15][16][35]
Why is it called kisspeptin?
The gene was discovered in Hershey, Pennsylvania, in 1996, and the name honors the town’s chocolate Kisses; the “SS” stands for suppressor sequence, because it was first found as a gene that suppressed cancer spread.[1][5]

Bottom line

Kisspeptin went from a cancer gene in 1996 to the recognized master switch of puberty, and it remains a valuable research tool and a promising IVF trigger.[1][4][15] As a testosterone or fertility booster, the record is thin: brief rises in small groups of healthy men, a response that fades with frequent use, suppression with constant exposure, and two terminated drug-company trials.[7][12][20][22] No product is approved, FDA flags compounded kisspeptin-10 as a safety risk, and it is banned in sport.[35][29][32] Men worried about low testosterone or fertility after TRT have approved options to discuss with a doctor; for what the trials show, see our pages on fertility recovery after testosterone and TRT in men over 40.
