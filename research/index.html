Study teardowns

Each teardown takes one study and reads it closely. We look at what was actually tested, what the comparator was, who funded and analyzed it, how dropout and missing data were handled, absolute versus relative effects, and who was enrolled. We also cover what the study robustly shows.

Every number comes from a source we opened and cite, with the date it was checked. Charts are rebuilt from the published tables, and figures we couldn't verify are left out. Educational only, not medical advice.

Latest

Wegovy (semaglutide) · Heart risk · SELECT trial, NEJM 2023

Does Wegovy (semaglutide) protect your heart if you don’t have diabetes? If you already have heart disease, yes: fewer heart attacks, strokes and cardiovascular deaths, with important caveats.

In people with heart disease but no diabetes, 6.5% on Wegovy vs 8.0% on placebo had a heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death over about 40 months. We cover what that means in absolute terms, who was studied, who paid for it, and how many stopped because of stomach side effects.

Longevity · Thymalin and Epithalamin extracts · Khavinson 2002–2003, translated

Will Epitalon and Thymalin make you live longer? We can’t really tell, so probably not.

Mortality halved in groups of 20–24, a non-significant result during treatment, a “4.1-fold” figure from a separate group of 20, the inventors running the trials — and an extract, not synthetic Epitalon.

Neuropeptide · Selank 2.7 mg/day intranasal vs phenazepam · Russian studies, translated

Can selank calm your anxiety without the drowsiness? It sedated far less than a benzodiazepine, but whether it works is unproven.

Far fewer side effects than phenazepam (sedation 0% vs 100%), but less anxiety relief on drug, an open-label design with no placebo, and funding from the manufacturer.

Neuropeptide · Semax 6,000 mcg/day intranasal · Russian studies, translated

Does semax help you recover from a stroke? Maybe, but the studies can’t really tell us.

Higher BDNF and faster Barthel gains in 20–30-patient subgroups, but no placebo, no described blinding, developer co-authors on the early work, and no replication outside Russia.

Zepbound/Mounjaro (tirzepatide) · Weight loss · SURMOUNT-1 trial, NEJM 2022

How much weight will you lose on Zepbound or Mounjaro (tirzepatide)? About 15–21% on average, depending on dose.

People lost 15.0%, 19.5% or 20.9% of body weight at 5, 10 or 15 mg over 72 weeks, vs 3.1% on placebo with the same lifestyle program. Lilly ran the analysis, and in a later trial people who switched to placebo regained much of the weight.

Wegovy (semaglutide) · Weight loss · STEP 1 trial, NEJM 2021

How much weight will you lose on Wegovy (semaglutide)? About 15% on average, and most of it came back after stopping.

People lost 14.9% of body weight over 68 weeks vs 2.4% on placebo. But the placebo group also got a lifestyle program, Novo Nordisk ran the analysis, and people regained about two-thirds of the lost weight within a year of stopping.

How we read a study

  • Design: randomization, blinding, registration, and prespecified endpoints.
  • Comparator: placebo, usual care, or an active drug, and what both arms actually received.
  • Funding and roles: who designed, analyzed, and wrote it.
  • Dropout and missing data: how many finished, and how the analysis handled the rest.
  • Effect size: absolute differences next to relative ones.
  • Population: who was enrolled and who was excluded.
  • Durability and replication: what happened later and in other studies.
