Study teardowns

Each teardown takes one study and reads it closely. We look at what was actually tested, what the comparator was, who funded and analyzed it, how dropout and missing data were handled, absolute versus relative effects, and who was enrolled. We also cover what the study robustly shows.

Every number comes from a source we opened and cite, with the date it was checked. Charts are rebuilt from the published tables, and figures we couldn't verify are left out. Educational only, not medical advice.

Latest

Wegovy, Ozempic (semaglutide) · Zepbound, Mounjaro (tirzepatide) · withdrawal trials, labels, registry review, October 2026

What happens when you stop Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro? In trials, half to two-thirds of the lost weight came back within a year, and in the one trial that checked, thoughts about food came back within 12 weeks.

In three randomized trials, people switched from Wegovy or Zepbound to placebo regained about half to two-thirds of their loss within a year, and blood pressure and A1C drifted back. A trial result posted only on ClinicalTrials.gov shows food thoughts back to baseline 12 weeks after stopping semaglutide. Stepping down to 5 mg Zepbound kept off more than stopping; tapering to zero has no results.

Zepbound, Mounjaro (tirzepatide) · Wegovy, Ozempic (semaglutide) · Saxenda (liraglutide) · exercise trials, Zepbound label, registry review, October 2026

Is cardio a waste of time unless you’re on Zepbound or Wegovy? Exercise still works without the drug, and on one it added fat loss. Nobody has tested cardio with tirzepatide.

A viral post says cardio only burns fat on tirzepatide. Hunger does eat back part of what exercise burns, but not all of it. In a year-long trial, exercise plus a GLP-1 drug cut body fat about twice as much as either alone, and exercise’s benefit outlasted the drug’s. 0 of 11 registered trials of semaglutide or tirzepatide with vs without exercise have results.

Ozempic, Wegovy (semaglutide) · Zepbound, Mounjaro (tirzepatide) · trial psychiatric data, FDA’s 2026 review, FAERS reports, registry count, October 2026

Do Ozempic, Wegovy and Zepbound dull your zest for life? In the big trials, mood and energy held up. Pleasure itself has barely been measured.

Nassim Taleb says GLP-1 users lose their gusto. In 7,400+ trial patients, depression scores ended slightly better than placebo and energy rose a little. But only 4 of 1,006 registered studies measure pleasure, all small addiction trials with no results, and the drugs do dim cravings beyond food.

SS-31 · elamipretide (Forzinity) · MTP-131 · FDA approval files, 13 placebo-controlled trials, FDA listings, October 2026

Does SS-31 recharge aging mitochondria? No placebo trial clearly says yes, and FDA’s own reviewers said no.

Thirteen placebo-controlled trials in heart, kidney, muscle and eye disease and older adults: 9 missed, 2 came close, 2 never reported. Its Barth syndrome approval overruled FDA’s clinical reviewers, X “microdoses” are as little as a twentieth of the approved dose, and 5 of 6 bulk-powder listings began after approval.

NAD+ IV drips and shots · Niagen (NR) injections · NMN · JAMA Oct 8 trial, Oct 14 preprint, FDA files, 1961–1973 papers, October 2026

Do NAD+ shots and IV drips slow aging? No placebo-controlled trial has tested that.

The five placebo tests of injected NAD+ measured comfort, blood levels or disease symptoms, never aging, and the largest used 10 mg a day against 500 mg clinic drips. Every drip client in one clinic review got cramps and nausea, and FDA’s 2019 plan to bar pharmacy NAD was never finished.

Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, BPC-157, retatrutide · FDA listing and registration files, import alerts, warning letters, October 2026

Who makes the powder in compounded Wegovy and Zepbound copies, BPC-157 and retatrutide? Mostly Chinese plants, and FDA’s files still list ones it flagged.

A Chinese plant FDA flagged for black mold and hidden documents still lists semaglutide and tirzepatide “for human prescription compounding.” Seven firms list retatrutide powder FDA says nobody may compound, and 15 of 20 BPC-157 powder listings began in 2026.

CJC-1295 + ipamorelin vs tesamorelin (Egrifta) · FDA votes, 2002 patent, ClinicalTrials.gov, PubMed count, October 2026

Does the CJC-1295 and ipamorelin stack work? Nobody has tested it in people, and FDA’s advisers voted against both halves.

The no-DAC CJC-1295 in most stacks has never been given to a person in a published study, ipamorelin was only tested by vein, and the pair was never tested together. FDA’s advisers rejected both in 2024, and neither is on the next agendas while a new peptide policy sits at the White House.

KPV · Lys-Pro-Val · 1984 papers, patents, Zengen trial records, FDA review, PubMed count, October 2026

Does KPV calm an inflamed gut? In mice, again and again. In people, nobody has published a test.

Independent labs found it eased colitis in mice, but in 42 years nobody has published a KPV trial in people. FDA reviewed it as a 0.1% skin cream that plain KPV can’t get through, and the oral doses on X are about 100 times below the mouse dose scaled to a person.

CagriSema · cagrilintide + semaglutide · FDA decision expected Q4 2026 · NEJM trials, SEC filings, October 2026

Is CagriSema better than Wegovy or Zepbound? Better than Wegovy, not Zepbound.

It added 5.5 points of weight loss over semaglutide alone, but lost to Zepbound head-to-head, 20.2% vs 23.6%. Only 57% stayed on the full dose, high-dose Wegovy closes most of the gap, and no trial has tested the gray-market cagrilintide stacks.

GHK-Cu · copper peptide · 1973 papers, SEC filings, 1994 press, FDA, PubMed count, October 2026

Does GHK-Cu rebuild aging skin or heal you from the inside? Its biggest trial failed, and nobody has tested the injections.

As a wound drug, GHK-Cu did no better than placebo in a 511-patient trial in 1994. The company then sold it as a dressing and a face cream. The “60% decline with age” figure has no source we could find, the “4,000 genes” came from two cancer cell lines, and nobody has tested injecting it.

MOTS-c · FDA review, patents, SEC filings, PubMed count, October 2026

Does MOTS-c work like exercise in a shot? In mice, partly. In people, nobody has shown it.

Mouse injections stopped weight gain and helped old mice run, at doses that scale to about 28 to 85 mg in a person. We read all 264 PubMed records and none gave MOTS-c to a human. The only company drug based on it was shelved, and the lifespan claim is a non-significant trend.

Foundayo · orforglipron · EU recommendation, US label, The Lancet, October 2026

Is the Foundayo (orforglipron) pill as good as Wegovy or Zepbound shots? Not for weight loss: about 11% versus 15% and 21%. But it’s the easiest GLP-1 to take, and with diabetes the gap mostly closes.

Europe’s regulator backed Lilly’s GLP-1 pill on October 15. On each US label, it adds 9 points of weight loss over placebo, versus 11 to 18 for its rivals, and more people quit it for side effects. With type 2 diabetes it kept more of its effect than Wegovy or Zepbound did, and the box dose isn’t the trial dose.

TB-500 · Thymosin beta-4 · FDA review, trial registries, racing labs, October 2026

Is TB-500 the same as thymosin beta-4, your body’s healing protein? No. It’s a 7-amino-acid piece that has never been tested in people.

It began as a 2003 NIH lab fragment and a racehorse product. The human trials people cite used the full protein, and the two biggest missed their main goals. TB-500’s only trial listing calls itself “fictional.”

BPC-157 · Origins · 1989 patent, PubMed and the human proteome, October 2026

Is BPC-157 a healing peptide your stomach already makes? Not exactly, and nearly all the research comes from one lab in Zagreb.

It’s a copy of a partial sequence from a protein one lab reported isolating in 1989, and we found it in none of 210,706 human protein sequences. 171 of 222 PubMed papers share one co-author, and the human data are one null colitis abstract plus 30 uncontrolled patients.

BPC-157 · Semax · Epitalon · FDA policy, October 2026 · Soviet sources

Can a pharmacy legally make you BPC-157, semax or epitalon now? Not yet, and FDA’s own scientists said the evidence isn’t there.

FDA sent a peptide compounding policy to the White House on October 14. Advisers backed six peptides 7–8 to 4–6; FDA staff opposed all of them. We read every briefing document and trace semax and epitalon back to their Soviet patents.

Retatrutide · Type 2 diabetes · TRIUMPH-2, The Lancet, October 2026

Will retatrutide work as well if you have type 2 diabetes? Not quite: about 19% weight loss, versus about 25% in people without diabetes.

18.8% at 12 mg vs 5.1% on placebo over 80 weeks. Why that’s below Lilly’s 28.3% headline, how it mirrors Wegovy’s diabetes gap, a new skin-sensation side effect, and what it means for gray-market vials.

Wegovy (semaglutide) · Heart risk · SELECT trial, NEJM 2023

Does Wegovy (semaglutide) protect your heart if you don’t have diabetes? If you already have heart disease, yes: fewer heart attacks, strokes and cardiovascular deaths, with important caveats.

In people with heart disease but no diabetes, 6.5% on Wegovy vs 8.0% on placebo had a heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death over about 40 months. We cover what that means in absolute terms, who was studied, who paid for it, and how many stopped because of stomach side effects.

Sleep and longevity · Epitalon, pinealon, thymalin · FDA 2026 review + Khavinson papers, translated

Will epitalon or pinealon fix your sleep, or help you live longer? We can’t tell, and nearly all the evidence comes from one St Petersburg lab.

FDA reviewed epitalon for insomnia and found no insomnia studies and no human safety data. Pinealon has no sleep trial at all. The famous mortality numbers come from groups of 20–24 run by the inventors.

Neuropeptide · Selank 2.7 mg/day intranasal vs phenazepam · Russian studies, translated

Can selank calm your anxiety without the drowsiness? It sedated far less than a benzodiazepine, but whether it works is unproven.

Far fewer side effects than phenazepam (sedation 0% vs 100%), but less anxiety relief on drug, an open-label design with no placebo, and funding from the manufacturer.

Neuropeptide · Semax 6,000 mcg/day intranasal · Russian studies, translated

Does semax help you recover from a stroke? Maybe, but the studies can’t really tell us.

Higher BDNF and faster Barthel gains in 20–30-patient subgroups, but no placebo, no described blinding, developer co-authors on the early work, and no replication outside Russia.

Zepbound/Mounjaro (tirzepatide) · Weight loss · SURMOUNT-1 trial, NEJM 2022

How much weight will you lose on Zepbound or Mounjaro (tirzepatide)? About 15–21% on average, depending on dose.

People lost 15.0%, 19.5% or 20.9% of body weight at 5, 10 or 15 mg over 72 weeks, vs 3.1% on placebo with the same lifestyle program. Lilly ran the analysis, and in a later trial people who switched to placebo regained much of the weight.

Wegovy (semaglutide) · Weight loss · STEP 1 trial, NEJM 2021

How much weight will you lose on Wegovy (semaglutide)? About 15% on average, and most of it came back after stopping.

People lost 14.9% of body weight over 68 weeks vs 2.4% on placebo. But the placebo group also got a lifestyle program, Novo Nordisk ran the analysis, and people regained about two-thirds of the lost weight within a year of stopping.

How we read a study

  • Design: randomization, blinding, registration, and prespecified endpoints.
  • Comparator: placebo, usual care, or an active drug, and what both arms actually received.
  • Funding and roles: who designed, analyzed, and wrote it.
  • Dropout and missing data: how many finished, and how the analysis handled the rest.
  • Effect size: absolute differences next to relative ones.
  • Population: who was enrolled and who was excluded.
  • Durability and replication: what happened later and in other studies.
