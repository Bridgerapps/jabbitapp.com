Amycretin, now zenagamtide (Novo Nordisk) · phase 1 and 2 trials in The Lancet · 15 phase 3 trials on ClinicalTrials.gov · JAMA on online sales, October 2026

Amycretin (zenagamtide): how much weight do people lose, and when could it be approved? Up to 22% to 24% in one small early trial, and 14.6% in a type 2 diabetes trial. It isn’t approved anywhere, and its first phase 3 results aren’t due until mid-2028.

Survodutide (BI 456906) · SYNCHRONIZE-1 and SYNCHRONIZE-2 in NEJM · vs Wegovy, Zepbound and retatrutide · heart-safety results due November 9, 2026

Survodutide: how much weight do people lose, and is it better than Zepbound or retatrutide? About 12% to 13% without diabetes and 8% to 10% with type 2 diabetes in Boehringer’s phase 3 trials, below Zepbound and retatrutide in separate trials.

Boehringer’s “up to 16.6%” and “13.1%” assume everyone stayed on the drug. With everyone counted, survodutide’s edge over placebo in type 2 diabetes is about the same as Wegovy’s. About one in five stopped the drug over stomach side effects, and we found no announced filing.

Is “Ozempic face” the drug or the weight loss? In the largest scan study we found, facial fat loss tracked weight lost.

The viral 70% cheek-fat figure comes from 5 patients in a 2023 meeting abstract who lost 21% of their weight. A 20-patient Vanderbilt study found a median 9% midface loss that rose with weight lost. Of 10 registered “Ozempic face” studies, 9 test a cosmetic fix, none has results, and none compares the drug with the same weight loss without it.

Testosterone enanthate and cypionate · anabolic steroids · “testmaxxing” · fertility, sperm recovery, steroid withdrawal · WHO contraception trials, Bhasin dose trials, former-user studies, ClinicalTrials.gov, October 2026

Injecting testosterone in your 20s or 30s without low T: will your sperm and your own testosterone come back? After short, supervised use, almost always. After self-directed steroid use, not always, and the one placebo-controlled recovery trial we found stopped after enrolling 8.

At 200 mg a week, 65% of healthy men had no sperm within 6 months; after stopping, counts recovered in 67% by 6 months, 90% by 12 and 100% by 24 (1,549 men, 30 studies). Lean mass rose 3.4 to 7.9 kg with dose. But 27% of former steroid users still had low testosterone about 2.5 years later, and the only placebo-controlled recovery trial enrolled 8 of 60 men before stopping.

Stopped losing weight on Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro? A plateau after about 6 to 8 months is normal. A higher dose or a switch to tirzepatide adds a few points, and retatrutide has no published trial in people switching.

Half of people on tirzepatide plateaued by 24 to 36 weeks. Wegovy 7.2 mg added 3.1 points over 2.4 mg; real switchers to tirzepatide lost 8.1% more after a plateau but 2.9% if semaglutide never worked. Do Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or CagriSema work after menopause, and does hormone therapy help? They work, with about 3 to 4 points less weight loss than before menopause. The hormone-therapy boost shrank as studies grew, and no trial has tested it.

On tirzepatide and CagriSema, women past menopause lost about 23% of their weight, versus 26 to 27% before. Across three Mayo Clinic record studies, the hormone-therapy edge went from 4 to 5 points in small groups to under 2 in a 2,379-woman cohort. Can Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro or Zepbound cause sudden vision loss? Possibly, but rarely: the biggest studies find about 3 to 10 extra cases of NAION per 10,000 users, Europe, the UK and Australia now warn about it, and US labels still don’t.

Large studies find NAION, an optic-nerve stroke, in 8.5 vs 5.5 per 10,000 GLP-1 users over 18 months, though Novo’s placebo-controlled trials show no increase. Does food noise come back on Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or retatrutide? Often, partly. In the longest trial that tracked it, the quiet faded by week 40, but people kept eating less.

Does microdosing Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro work? Lower doses do something, but the “microdoses” people buy have never been tested in a randomized trial, and the new “low-dose” heart study used about a full Wegovy dose.

Daily semaglutide at 0.05 mg beat placebo, but tirzepatide at 1 mg a week, inside the usual microdose range, cut just 0.9 kg vs 0.4 kg. The Cedars-Sinai “low-dose” heart study’s rat dose scales to about 2.4 mg a week, the full Wegovy dose. Of 15 registered low-dose studies, none has posted results, and four pen instructions say not to count clicks.

Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus (semaglutide) · Zepbound, Mounjaro (tirzepatide) · bone density, osteoporosis, fractures · randomized trials, 9 FDA labels, ClinicalTrials.gov, October 2026

Do Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro weaken your bones? Hip bone density drops a little as the weight comes off, fracture data are mixed, and tirzepatide has the newest warning signs and the least testing.

For World Osteoporosis Day: semaglutide left hip density 0.020 g/cm² below placebo in a year, while liraglutide plus exercise kept it at placebo levels. Wegovy’s label reports hip and pelvis fractures in 2.4% vs 0.6% of people 75 and older, but it is the only one of 9 GLP-1 labels to mention fractures. Of 30 registered bone studies, 2 have results and none tests tirzepatide against placebo.

Retatrutide (“Reta”) · BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295, GHK-Cu · NSW Health emergency department data, TGA and MHRA findings, FDA adverse event reports, October 2026

Should Australia’s 1,180 peptide ER visits worry anyone using gray-market retatrutide? The count proves mentions, not harm, but monthly visits rose from 5 to 217 in 15 months, skew young, and matches lab tests of vials holding the wrong dose or the wrong drug.

NSW Health counted 1,180 ER visits mentioning unapproved peptides from July 2025 to September 2026, 833 naming retatrutide. We read the monthly numbers out of the report’s chart: admissions held at 24 to 30 a month since May while visits kept rising. 57% were under 35, against 9% of the state’s GLP-1 users. Retatrutide · tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) · FDA’s October 2 decision, Seventh Circuit appeal, patent, generics and compounding rules, October 2026

Why is Lilly suing FDA to call retatrutide a biologic? The label mostly decides how long it stays copy-free after approval, and whether anyone can ever legally compound it.

FDA ruled again on October 2 that retatrutide is a drug: 39 alpha amino acids in its chain, one short of the line Lilly needs. As a biologic, no copy could be approved for 12 years and compounding would be shut for good; as a drug, copies could apply at year 4. But the patent covering its exact structure runs to about January 2039 either way, so the label matters most if that patent falls.

Ozempic · Wegovy · Zepbound · Mounjaro · COURAGE, BELIEVE, EMBRAZE and QUALITY muscle-drug trials, MRI studies, protein and creatine evidence, ClinicalTrials.gov, October 2026

Muscle loss on Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro: about a quarter to a third of the weight lost is lean mass. Lifting and protein are what guidelines back; creatine and the new muscle drugs are unproven on these drugs.

In four recent trials, 29% to 34% of the weight lost on semaglutide or tirzepatide alone was DXA lean mass, about what diet or surgery does, and MRI shows thigh muscle shrinking roughly in line with the weight lost. Experimental muscle drugs cut that share to about 8–18%, but none registered a strength test and none is approved. Testosterone (TRT) · TRAVERSE and T Trials · FDA’s June 2026 label request · DailyMed label check, October 2026

Is testosterone therapy (TRT) a “wonder drug” for men over 40? In the biggest trials it helped sex drive, anemia and mood a little, did little for energy or strength, and came with more fractures and heart-rhythm problems.

Alan Ritchson’s “wonder drug” TRT praise passed 640,000 views on X. In TRAVERSE, with 5,246 men, heart attacks and strokes didn’t rise (7.0% vs 7.3%), but fractures (3.5% vs 2.5%), atrial fibrillation and kidney injury did. A year of gel didn’t improve energy or walking in men 65 and older. FDA asked in June to drop the line saying it’s unproven for aging men, and 14 of 15 labels published since still carry it.

Ozempic · Wegovy · Zepbound · Mounjaro · ANESTHESIOLOGY 2026 records study, OCULUS randomized trial, stomach ultrasound studies, 7 guidelines, ClinicalTrials.gov, October 2026

Should you stop Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro before surgery? U.S. guidance now says most people can keep taking it, but the one randomized test found food left in the stomach far more often when people didn’t skip a dose.

The new 2-million-surgery study’s “11% lower odds” is 1.0% vs 1.1% for heart complications, and it didn’t track who held a dose. Ozempic · Wegovy · Rybelsus · Zepbound · Mounjaro · Novo preprint, Lilly conference data, independent trials, ClinicalTrials.gov, October 2026

Do Ozempic, Wegovy and Zepbound slow aging? Some blood-protein aging clocks read younger in sick, mostly diabetic patients, but that is not proof anyone ages slower.

Novo’s 10,052-person aging-clock analysis was 80% people with type 2 diabetes, and more than half took the Rybelsus pill. In SELECT, the Wegovy trial, 3 of 21 clocks read younger at week 20 and the fat-tissue clock read older. The whole-body clock didn’t move in people without diabetes. Lilly’s tirzepatide data had no placebo, and none of the 8 registered GLP-1 aging-clock trials has results.

What happens when you stop Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound or Mounjaro? In trials, half to two-thirds of the lost weight came back within a year, and in the one trial that checked, thoughts about food came back within 12 weeks.

A viral post says cardio only burns fat on tirzepatide. Hunger does eat back part of what exercise burns, but not all of it. Do Ozempic, Wegovy and Zepbound dull your zest for life? In the big trials, mood and energy held up. Pleasure itself has barely been measured.

Nassim Taleb says GLP-1 users lose their gusto. In 7,400+ trial patients, depression scores ended slightly better than placebo and energy rose a little. But only 4 of 1,006 registered studies measure pleasure, all small addiction trials with no results, and the drugs do dim cravings beyond food.

Is CagriSema better than Wegovy or Zepbound? Better than Wegovy, not Zepbound.

It added 5.5 points of weight loss over semaglutide alone, but lost to Zepbound head-to-head, 20.2% vs 23.6%. Only 57% stayed on the full dose, high-dose Wegovy closes most of the gap, and no trial has tested the gray-market cagrilintide stacks.

Is the Foundayo (orforglipron) pill as good as Wegovy or Zepbound shots? Not for weight loss: about 11% versus 15% and 21%. But it’s the easiest GLP-1 to take, and with diabetes the gap mostly closes.

Europe’s regulator backed Lilly’s GLP-1 pill on October 15. On each US label, it adds 9 points of weight loss over placebo, versus 11 to 18 for its rivals, and more people quit it for side effects. With type 2 diabetes it kept more of its effect than Wegovy or Zepbound did, and the box dose isn’t the trial dose.

Does Wegovy (semaglutide) protect your heart if you don’t have diabetes? If you already have heart disease, yes: fewer heart attacks, strokes and cardiovascular deaths, with important caveats.

In people with heart disease but no diabetes, 6.5% on Wegovy vs 8.0% on placebo had a heart attack, stroke or cardiovascular death over about 40 months. We cover what that means in absolute terms, who was studied, who paid for it, and how many stopped because of stomach side effects.

Zepbound/Mounjaro (tirzepatide) · Weight loss · SURMOUNT-1 trial, NEJM 2022

How much weight will you lose on Zepbound or Mounjaro (tirzepatide)? About 15–21% on average, depending on dose.

People lost 15.0%, 19.5% or 20.9% of body weight at 5, 10 or 15 mg over 72 weeks, vs 3.1% on placebo with the same lifestyle program. Lilly ran the analysis, and in a later trial people who switched to placebo regained much of the weight.

Wegovy (semaglutide) · Weight loss · STEP 1 trial, NEJM 2021

How much weight will you lose on Wegovy (semaglutide)? About 15% on average, and most of it came back after stopping.

People lost 14.9% of body weight over 68 weeks vs 2.4% on placebo. But the placebo group also got a lifestyle program, Novo Nordisk ran the analysis, and people regained about two-thirds of the lost weight within a year of stopping.

How we read a study

  • Design: randomization, blinding, registration, and prespecified endpoints.
  • Comparator: placebo, usual care, or an active drug, and what both arms actually received.
  • Funding and roles: who designed, analyzed, and wrote it.
  • Dropout and missing data: how many finished, and how the analysis handled the rest.
  • Effect size: absolute differences next to relative ones.
  • Population: who was enrolled and who was excluded.
  • Durability and replication: what happened later and in other studies.
