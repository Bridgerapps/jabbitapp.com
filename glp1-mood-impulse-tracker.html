GLP-1 Mood and Impulse Tracker: Sleep, Cravings, Urges

Mood, sleep, cravings, anxiety, and impulse control can shift for many reasons. A GLP-1 timeline helps separate dose context from stress, under-eating, alcohol, sleep debt, and life changes.

Track mood, urges, sleep, and dose timing in Jabbit
Escalate serious symptoms: Severe depression, suicidal thoughts, mania-like behavior, dangerous spending, or loss of control is not a tracking problem. Seek urgent professional support.

What regulators say

FDA and EMA reviews have not found evidence supporting a causal link between GLP-1 receptor agonists and suicidal thoughts/actions, while monitoring continues. That means content should avoid causal panic but still support practical symptom tracking.
