Safety · Australian coroner · October 29, 2026

Coroner links CJC-1295/ipamorelin to a fatal allergic reaction

A coroner in Victoria found an injection of the premixed peptides triggered actor Richard Norton’s fatal anaphylaxis in 2025, and asked Australia’s TGA to consider tighter limits. One case; asthma and a heart condition contributed.

Company results · Lilly earnings · October 29, 2026

Lilly’s Q3 2026: Mounjaro’s heart label, Zepbound and Foundayo sales

Lilly reported $10.7 billion of Mounjaro, $5.3 billion of US Zepbound and $184 million of Foundayo revenue for the quarter, and listed Mounjaro’s August approval to cut heart risk in type 2 diabetes. Sales are not efficacy data.

Regulatory · HHS notice · October 28, 2026

HHS makes FDA’s peptide compounding policy public

A draft enforcement discretion policy for pharmacies compounding six peptides, BPC-157, epitalon, KPV, MOTS-c, semax and TB-500, under conditions. HHS filed it for Federal Register publication on October 30, open to comment. It does not make any of them an approved drug.

Clinical trial · Lilly topline · October 28, 2026

Retatrutide vs Ozempic: the first head-to-head trial (TRANSCEND-T2D-2)

Lilly reported about 20% weight loss vs 9.4% on Ozempic 2 mg and A1C down 2.2 vs 1.8 points at 80 weeks, on its efficacy estimand. It is a press release, not a published paper, and retatrutide is not approved.

Legal · Court records · October 27, 2026

Lilly’s retatrutide lawsuits: status of all 11 cases

Lilly has sued 11 sellers and clinics over retatrutide. Three cases have ended, eight are open, and no court has decided whether Lilly’s claims are right. The suits target sellers, not buyers.

Clinical trial · Vanda Phase 3 · October 27, 2026

Tradipitant (NEREUS) for GLP-1 nausea and vomiting

Vanda Pharmaceuticals reported that tradipitant cut vomiting from 58.5% to 43.0% in its Phase 3 trial. It is approved only for motion sickness, and the trial used a harsher-than-normal dose start.

Legal · Reuters report · October 26, 2026

Judge declines to halt Lilly’s Zepbound vs Wegovy ads

Reuters reported that a federal judge in New Jersey denied Novo Nordisk’s request for a preliminary injunction against Lilly’s comparison ads; the lawsuit continues. What SURMOUNT-5 and a 41,381-person meta-analysis show.

Research · Heart Rhythm · October 26, 2026

94-trial analysis ranks semaglutide best for AFib reports

A network meta-analysis of 94 randomized trials found 18% fewer atrial fibrillation reports on injected semaglutide than on placebo. AF was mostly logged as a side effect, and certainty is rated low.

Clinical trial · NEJM · October 21, 2026

Retatrutide sleep apnea trials published

Two Lilly trials in NEJM found retatrutide cut breathing events more than placebo, by less in people with type 2 diabetes. The effect without diabetes looks similar to Zepbound’s; there is no head-to-head trial.

Hanmi’s weekly GLP-1 shot was approved as the first Korean-developed GLP-1 obesity drug. Its label reports 9.02% weight loss vs 0.89% on placebo at 40 weeks. It is not FDA approved.
