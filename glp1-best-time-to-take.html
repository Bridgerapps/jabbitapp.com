Best Time to Take GLP-1 Medications

Morning vs night — what works best for Mounjaro, Ozempic, Zepbound?

Morning vs Night

Most people prefer morning injection — here's why:

  • Can monitor side effects during the day
  • Less disruption to sleep
  • Establish a consistent routine

With Food or Without?

Take on empty stomach — either first thing in the morning or at least 2 hours after eating.

GLP-1 slows digestion. Taking with food can increase nausea.

Tips for Success

  1. Pick a day — same day each week
  2. Pick a time — morning works best for most
  3. Set a reminder — don't miss doses
  4. Stay hydrated — helps with side effects
