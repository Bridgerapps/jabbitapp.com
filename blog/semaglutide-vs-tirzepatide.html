Semaglutide vs Tirzepatide: Which GLP-1 Is Right for You?

The two hottest weight loss medications on the market share similar names but work differently. Here's the data-driven comparison.

The Basics

  • Semaglutide - Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus (GLP-1 receptor agonist)
  • Tirzepatide - Mounjaro, Zepbound (GLP-1 + GIP dual agonist)

Efficacy Comparison

Based on clinical trials:

  • Semaglutide 2.4mg: ~15% weight loss at 68 weeks
  • Tirzepatide 15mg: ~20% weight loss at 72 weeks
  • Tirzepatide wins on raw efficacy

Onset Speed

  • Semaglutide: Gradual onset, builds over weeks
  • Tirzepatide: Faster initial response, more dramatic early results

Side Effect Profile

Both cause GI issues, but:

  • Semaglutide: Generally milder, more predictable
  • Tirzepatide: Can be more intense initially, but often subsides

Cost Comparison

  • Semaglutide: ~$900-1400/month (brand dependent)
  • Tirzepatide: ~$1000-1500/month
  • Compounded versions available at lower cost

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Semaglutide if:

  • You want a more established track record
  • You have type 2 diabetes (semaglutide has better diabetes data)
  • You're sensitive to medication side effects

Choose Tirzepatide if:

  • Maximum weight loss is your goal
  • You've tried semaglutide without success
  • You can handle more aggressive dosing

The Best Approach

Start with semaglutide. If you've plateaued after 3-6 months at maintenance dose, consider switching to tirzepatide. Many find they need both at different phases of their journey.
