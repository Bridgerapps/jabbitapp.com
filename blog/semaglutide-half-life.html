How Long Does Semaglutide Stay in Your System?

Understanding semaglutide's half-life helps you plan doses, understand side effects, and know what to expect when stopping.

The Numbers

  • Half-life: ~7 days (165-184 hours)
  • Time to steady state: 4-5 weeks
  • Time to clear: ~5-7 weeks after last dose

What This Means

1. Weekly Dosing

The 7-day half-life is why semaglutide is dosed weekly. Each dose adds to the previous, building to a steady state.

2. Steady State

After 4-5 weeks of consistent dosing, the medication reaches steady state - meaning the amount you're taking equals the amount being cleared.

3. After Stopping

It takes about 5-7 weeks for semaglutide to fully clear from your system after the last dose. This is why side effects can persist for weeks after stopping.

Why Half-Life Matters

Dosing Decisions

If you miss a dose by a few days, you don't need to restart. The medication is still in your system.

Side Effects

Side effects often peak when you first start and improve as your body adjusts. But they can also return if you escalate doses too quickly.

Switching Medications

If switching from semaglutide to tirzepatide, you need to account for the overlap. They work similarly but tirzepatide is more potent.

Surgery Considerations

Tell your doctor if you've taken semaglutide before any surgery. The delayed gastric emptying can pose risks with anesthesia.

Comparison with Other GLP-1s

  • Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy): ~7 days
  • Tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound): ~5 days
  • Liraglutide (Victoza): ~13 hours
  • Dulaglutide (Trulicity): ~5 days
