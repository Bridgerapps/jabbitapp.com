How to Track GLP-1 Injections: The Complete Guide

Tracking your GLP-1 injections isn't just about remembering when to take your medication. It's about understanding how your body responds, identifying patterns, and optimizing your results.

Why Track GLP-1 Injections?

Here's what tracking reveals that memory cannot:

  • Side effect patterns - When do nausea and GI symptoms hit hardest?
  • Weight loss correlation - How does each dose affect the scale?
  • Appetite cycles - When does the medication wear off?
  • Site rotation - Are you alternating injection locations?

What to Track

At minimum, track these fields:

  • Date and time of injection
  • Dose amount (mg or units)
  • Injection site (abdomen, thigh, arm)
  • Weight that day
  • Appetite rating (1-10)
  • Side effects (if any)
  • Food notes

The Problem with Spreadsheets

Most people start with spreadsheets. But spreadsheets don't remind you when to inject. They can't calculate when your next dose should be. And they certainly can't visualize your serum levels over time.

The Smarter Approach

Apps designed for peptide and hormone tracking offer:

  • Automatic dose calculations
  • Injection site rotation reminders
  • Weight trend visualization
  • Cycle planning (on/off weeks)
  • Serum level estimates

Key Metrics That Matter

Don't just track - analyze. These metrics will help you and your doctor optimize your protocol:

  • Weeks to goal weight - Are you on track?
  • Dose-response curve - Is higher dose = more weight loss?
  • Side effect frequency - Are they decreasing over time?
  • Injection site issues - Any lumps or irritation?

Best Practices

  1. Track immediately - Don't wait until end of day
  2. Be consistent - Same time each week
  3. Note context - What did you eat? How did you sleep?
  4. Review weekly - Look for patterns
  5. Share with doctor - Bring data to appointments

Conclusion

Whether you use an app or a notebook, tracking your GLP-1 journey transforms it from guesswork into science. The data you collect today becomes the insight that optimizes tomorrow.
