Peptide Site Rotation Guide: Prevent Lipodystrophy

New to peptides? Don't make the mistake that ruins thousands of protocols: injecting in the same spot repeatedly.

What Happens Without Rotation

  • Lipodystrophy - Fat redistribution causing lumps or divots
  • Poor absorption - Scar tissue doesn't absorb well
  • Increased pain - Damaged tissue is more sensitive
  • Irritation and scarring

The 14-Zone System

Divide your body into 14 zones and rotate through them:

Abdomen (6 zones)

  • Upper left, 2 inches from navel
  • Upper right, 2 inches from navel
  • Mid left, 2 inches from navel
  • Mid right, 2 inches from navel
  • Lower left, 2 inches from navel
  • Lower right, 2 inches from navel

Thighs (4 zones)

  • Upper outer left
  • Upper outer right
  • Upper inner left
  • Upper inner right

Arms (4 zones)

  • Upper outer left arm
  • Upper outer right arm
  • Lower outer left arm
  • Lower outer right arm

Best Practices

  1. Wait 7 days before returning to same zone
  2. Move clockwise to create consistent habit
  3. Log every injection - don't rely on memory
  4. Check sites weekly for any lumps
  5. Space 2 inches minimum between injection points
