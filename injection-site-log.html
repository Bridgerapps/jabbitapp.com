Keep a log of your injection sites

If your prescriber or pharmacist has told you to change where you inject, Jabbit gives you a place to record the site you chose, so you can look back at what you did. It doesn’t tell you which site to use.

Jabbit is an injection log for iPhone: injections, vials and sites in one place.

Download on the App Store

Record the site with each entry

When you log an injection, add the site. Over time you get a plain history of where and when, in the order you entered it.

Look back before your next one

Scroll your history to see which sites you’ve recorded recently. Jabbit shows past entries only; it doesn’t suggest a next site or a schedule.

Follow your care team’s instructions

Which sites to use, and how to rotate them, comes from your prescriber and the instructions for your medicine. Jabbit only keeps the record.

Everything in one place

Site entries sit alongside your injections and vials. See the full list on the features page, or read more in our guides.
