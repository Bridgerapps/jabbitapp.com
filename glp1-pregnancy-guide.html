GLP-1 Pregnancy & Breastfeeding Guide

Everything you need to know about using Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound during pregnancy and breastfeeding

One of the most common questions from people using GLP-1 medications is what happens if you become pregnant or want to breastfeed while on these medications. This guide covers everything you need to know about GLP-1s and pregnancy, including safety considerations, what the research says, and practical steps to take.

Important: This guide is for informational purposes only. Always consult with your healthcare provider before making any changes to your medication regimen, especially when planning pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Can You Use GLP-1 Medications During Pregnancy?

The short answer is: GLP-1 medications are not recommended during pregnancy. Here's what you need to know:

Current Medical Recommendations

  • FDA Pregnancy Category: GLP-1 medications are not recommended for use during pregnancy. Women of childbearing potential should use effective contraception while using these medications.
  • Manufacturer Guidelines: Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound all recommend discontinuing the medication at least 2 months before planned pregnancy.
  • Animal Studies: Some GLP-1 medications have shown adverse effects in animal reproduction studies, though animal results don't always predict human outcomes.
Key Point: If you become pregnant while on a GLP-1 medication, don't panic. Contact your healthcare provider immediately. They will help you develop a safe plan for discontinuing the medication and managing your blood sugar during pregnancy.

What Happens When You Stop GLP-1 for Pregnancy?

When you stop GLP-1 medications to conceive or during pregnancy, you may experience:

  • Weight regain: Some weight you lost may return as the medication's appetite-suppressing effects wear off
  • Blood sugar changes: If you were using GLP-1 for type 2 diabetes, your blood sugar management may change
  • Return of hunger signals: Normal hunger cues will return, which can be challenging to navigate
Good News: Many people successfully stop GLP-1 medications and maintain their pregnancy weight goals through diet, exercise, and working with their healthcare team. The healthy habits you developed while on GLP-1 therapy can continue to serve you well during pregnancy.

Planning Pregnancy While on GLP-1 Therapy

If you're currently on GLP-1 medication and considering pregnancy, here's a practical approach:

Steps to Take

  1. Talk to your provider: Discuss your pregnancy plans with the healthcare provider who prescribed your GLP-1 medication
  2. Create a timeline: Work together to create a safe discontinuation timeline
  3. Establish alternative management: If you have type 2 diabetes or PCOS, discuss pregnancy-safe alternatives
  4. Focus on fundamentals: Use your time on GLP-1 therapy to build healthy habits that will support pregnancy
  5. Monitor nutrition: Ensure you're getting adequate nutrients for pregnancy preparation

GLP-1 and Fertility: What the Research Shows

Emerging research suggests GLP-1 medications may actually improve fertility for some people:

  • Weight loss: Achieving a healthier weight can improve fertility outcomes
  • PCOS improvement: GLP-1s may help with PCOS symptoms, which can improve ovulation
  • Metabolic health: Better blood sugar control may support reproductive health
Research Note: While some studies suggest GLP-1 medications may improve fertility markers, these medications are not approved for fertility treatment. Always work with a fertility specialist or OB-GYN when planning pregnancy.

Common Questions

Can I get pregnant while on Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro?

Yes, it's possible to become pregnant while on GLP-1 medications. However, these medications are not recommended during pregnancy. If you become pregnant, contact your healthcare provider immediately to discuss discontinuing the medication safely.

How long after stopping GLP-1 can I try to conceive?

Most healthcare providers recommend waiting 2-3 months after stopping GLP-1 medications before trying to conceive. This allows time for the medication to clear from your system and for your body to return to its normal function.

Will my weight come back if I stop GLP-1 for pregnancy?

Some weight regain is possible when stopping GLP-1 medications. However, many people maintain their progress through healthy eating and exercise. Focus on nourishing your body during pregnancy rather than weight loss.

Can I use GLP-1 while trying to conceive?

Most providers recommend discontinuing GLP-1 medications before trying to conceive. Discuss your specific situation with your healthcare provider to determine the best approach for you.

What if I have gestational diabetes? Can I use GLP-1?

GLP-1 medications are not currently approved for gestational diabetes treatment. If you develop gestational diabetes, your healthcare provider will recommend pregnancy-safe alternatives such as insulin or oral medications like metformin.

Can I restart GLP-1 after pregnancy?

Yes, many people successfully restart GLP-1 medications after pregnancy and breastfeeding. Discuss timing with your provider, especially if you're breastfeeding.

Key Takeaways

  • GLP-1 medications are not recommended during pregnancy - discontinue 2-3 months before trying to conceive
  • Breastfeeding while on GLP-1 is not recommended due to unknown effects on breast milk
  • Always consult your healthcare provider before making any changes to your medication regimen
  • Healthy habits developed on GLP-1 therapy can support a healthy pregnancy
  • Work with your provider to create a personalized plan for pregnancy and postpartum
