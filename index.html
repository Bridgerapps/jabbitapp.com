FAQ

Quick answers to the most common questions before you download.

Does Jabbit work for GLP‑1 meds and other injection routines? ▾
Jabbit is built for GLP‑1 routines (for example semaglutide or tirzepatide) and also works well as a simple, private log for other injectable schedules and multi‑step protocols. Always follow your prescriber’s instructions. If you’re deciding what to track, these guides may help: GLP‑1 dosing schedules and injection tracking basics.
Do I need to create an account? ▾
No. Download and start tracking — no email, no sign‑up. Sync happens through your iCloud account.
Where is my data stored? ▾
Your logs are stored on your devices and sync through your personal iCloud account. We don’t run a server that stores your personal logs.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store, and you can cancel at any time from your Apple ID settings.
Is this medical advice? ▾
No. Jabbit provides educational information and tracking tools — not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
