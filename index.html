What the app does

Jabbit logs your injections and sites, reminds you at the times you set, tracks the vials you draw from, shows a modelled level estimate from the doses you log, up to now (an estimate, not a measurement), and records weight and progress photos.

Schedule & reminders

Enter the schedule your prescriber gave you and get reminders at the times you set.

Vial log

Log your vials and see the amount left, worked out from the doses you log.

Modelled level estimate

See a modelled level estimate from the doses you log, up to now. An estimate, not a measurement.

FAQ

Does Jabbit work for my injection schedule?▾
Jabbit logs the injections your prescriber has planned, including weekly and multi‑step schedules. Always follow your prescriber’s instructions. If you’re deciding what to track, see the injection tracking guide.
Can I export or delete my records?▾
Yes. You can export your records to a file from Settings, or delete everything, at any time.
How much does it cost?▾
Free download; a Jabbit Pro subscription is required: $9.99 a year (US App Store, checked November 3, 2026). It renews automatically every year until you cancel. Manage or cancel it anytime in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
Is this medical advice?▾
No. Jabbit provides educational information and tracking tools — not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
