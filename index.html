FAQ

Does Jabbit work for GLP‑1 meds and other injection routines?▾
Jabbit is built for GLP‑1 routines (for example semaglutide or tirzepatide) and also works as a private log for other injectable schedules and multi‑step protocols. Always follow your prescriber’s instructions. If you’re deciding what to track, see the injection tracking guide.
Do I need to create an account?▾
No. Download and start tracking — no email, no sign‑up. Sync happens through your iCloud account.
Where is my data stored?▾
Your logs are stored on your devices and sync through your personal iCloud account. We don’t run a server that stores your personal logs.
How much does it cost?▾
A 14‑day free trial, then $9.99/year. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store, and you can cancel at any time from your Apple ID settings.
Is this medical advice?▾
No. Jabbit provides educational information and tracking tools — not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
