A testosterone tracker that's just a logbook

If your prescriber has you on testosterone injections, Jabbit gives you one place to record them: date, time, site, vial and your own notes.

Jabbit is an injection log for iPhone: injections, vials and sites in one place.

Download on the App Store

What you can record

  • The date and time of each injection
  • The site you used
  • The vial it came from
  • Notes in your own words

Reminders at the times you set

Choose your own reminder times. Jabbit reminds you; it doesn’t set or change a schedule.

Your history at a glance

Scroll back through past entries whenever you need them. Jabbit shows what you entered and doesn’t interpret it. It doesn't track lab results or estimate testosterone levels. See also the injection site log and injection reminders.

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants a written record of their prescribed injections. Questions about your medicine belong with your prescriber.
