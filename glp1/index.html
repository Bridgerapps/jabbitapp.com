GLP-1 tracking in Jabbit

Jabbit is an iPhone app for people taking GLP-1 medications such as semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound). It reminds you on your dosing schedule, logs each dose and injection site, and charts your progress.

Smart Reminders

Set a daily, weekly or custom schedule for each medication, matching what your prescriber set.

Weight Correlation

Track weight and BMI, with Apple Health sync, alongside your dose log.

Side Effect Logging

Note nausea, appetite changes and other effects next to your doses, so you can show your prescriber.

Private by Design

Your logs are stored on your devices and your private iCloud. There is no Jabbit account and no Jabbit server holding your logs.
