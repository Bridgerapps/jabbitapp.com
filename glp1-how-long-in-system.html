How Long Does GLP-1 Stay in Your System?

Half-life and duration for Mounjaro, Ozempic, Zepbound

Medication Half-Lives

Tirzepatide: ~5 days
Semaglutide: ~7 days

Half-life = time for half the drug to leave your body.

Full Duration

Tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound):

  • 5-6 half-lives = ~25-30 days to fully clear

Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy):

  • 5-6 half-lives = ~35-42 days to fully clear

What This Means

  • Build-up: Weekly doses accumulate for first 4-6 weeks
  • Steady state: After ~5 doses, levels stabilize
  • After stopping: Effects linger ~4-5 weeks
