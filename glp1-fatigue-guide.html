GLP-1 Fatigue: Why You Feel Tired on Ozempic, Wegovy & Mounjaro

Feeling unusually tired while on GLP-1 medications like Ozempic, Wegovy, or Mounjaro? You're not alone. Fatigue is one of the most commonly reported side effects of these weight loss and diabetes medications.

Why Does GLP-1 Cause Fatigue?

There are several reasons GLP-1 medications can make you feel tired:

  • Caloric Restriction: These medications reduce appetite and food intake, which can initially lead to lower energy levels
  • Metabolic Adaptation: Your body is adjusting to a new metabolic state
  • Blood Sugar Fluctuations: Especially in diabetic patients, blood sugar changes can cause fatigue
  • GI Side Effects: Nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea can drain energy
  • Hormone Changes: GLP-1 affects multiple hormones that influence energy

How Long Does Fatigue Last?

For most people, GLP-1 fatigue is temporary:

  • Week 1-2: Most common, as your body adjusts
  • Week 3-4: Often improves significantly
  • After Month 1: Usually resolves or becomes manageable

Tips to Combat GLP-1 Fatigue

  1. Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water throughout the day
  2. Eat Protein: Prioritize protein-rich foods to maintain energy
  3. Small Frequent Meals: Avoid large meals that can cause blood sugar spikes
  4. Get Enough Sleep: Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep
  5. Light Exercise: Gentle movement can boost energy levels
  6. Check B12: Deficiency can worsen fatigue

When to Contact Your Doctor

While mild fatigue is normal, contact your healthcare provider if:

  • Fatigue is severe or persistent beyond 4 weeks
  • You experience dizziness or weakness
  • You have symptoms of dehydration
  • Fatigue significantly impacts your daily life

Is GLP-1 Fatigue Normal?

Yes, fatigue is a well-documented and common side effect of GLP-1 medications. Studies show approximately 20-30% of users experience some level of fatigue, particularly in the first few weeks of treatment.

FAQ

Does Wegovy cause more fatigue than Ozempic?
All GLP-1 medications can cause fatigue. There's no definitive evidence that one causes more fatigue than others.
Can I take supplements for GLP-1 fatigue?
B-complex vitamins, especially B12, may help. Always consult your doctor before starting supplements.
Will fatigue go away on its own?
For most people, yes. Fatigue typically improves within 4-6 weeks as your body adjusts.
Does dosage affect fatigue?
Higher doses may increase fatigue initially. Working with your doctor to find the right dose can help.
