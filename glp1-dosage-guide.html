GLP-1 Dosage Guide

Find Your Optimal Dose & Maximize Results

💡 Key Insight: Most people start at a low dose and gradually increase. Finding your optimal dose is a process—track your response to dial it in perfectly.

Understanding GLP-1 Titration

GLP-1 medications like Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Mounjaro®, and Zepbound® work best when you find the right dose for your body. This typically involves a gradual increase called "titration."

Standard Dosing Schedules

Medication Starting Dose Standard Progression Maximum Dose
Ozempic® 0.25 mg/week 0.25 → 0.5 → 1.0 → 2.0 mg 2.0 mg/week
Wegovy® 0.25 mg/week 0.25 → 0.5 → 1.0 → 1.7 → 2.4 mg 2.4 mg/week
Mounjaro® 2.5 mg/week 2.5 → 5.0 → 7.5 → 10 → 12.5 → 15 mg 15 mg/week
Zepbound® 2.5 mg/week 2.5 → 5.0 → 7.5 → 10 → 12.5 → 15 mg 15 mg/week

How to Know If Your Dose Is Right

  • Appetite suppression: Mild reduction in hunger without feeling deprived
  • Blood sugar control: Stable glucose levels (if diabetic)
  • Weight loss: Consistent 1-2 lbs per week after initial adjustment
  • Tolerable side effects: Nausea manageable, no severe symptoms
  • Sustained energy: No excessive fatigue or brain fog
⚠️ Important: Never adjust your dose without consulting your healthcare provider. They can help you find the right dose based on your response and tolerability.

Signs You May Need a Dose Adjustment

Too Low:

  • Minimal appetite suppression
  • Little to no weight loss after 4+ weeks
  • Cravings return quickly after meals

Too High:

  • Severe nausea or vomiting
  • Persistent diarrhea or constipation
  • Fatigue that's affecting daily life
  • Food aversion making it hard to eat enough protein

Tips for Successful Dosing

  1. Track everything: Log your dose date, hunger levels, food intake, weight, and side effects
  2. Stay at each dose: Give your body 4 weeks to adjust before judging effectiveness
  3. Inject consistently: Same day and time each week for stable blood levels
  4. Eat protein-first: Prioritize protein to prevent muscle loss
  5. Stay hydrated: GLP-1s can cause dehydration
  6. Communicate with your provider: Share your tracking data at appointments

Common Dosage Questions

How long should I stay at each dose?

Most people stay at each dose for 4 weeks. If side effects are severe, your provider may have you stay longer at a lower dose before increasing.

What if I miss a dose?

If it's been less than 5 days since your scheduled dose, take it as soon as you remember. If it's been more than 5 days, skip that dose and take your next scheduled dose.

Can I split my dose?

Some people split doses to reduce side effects, but always consult your provider first. The medication needs to be injected subcutaneously.

Will higher doses work better?

Not necessarily. Higher doses increase side effects without necessarily improving weight loss. The goal is the lowest effective dose that produces results.
