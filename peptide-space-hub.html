What to log in any peptide tracker

A useful page in this cluster should help someone preserve enough detail to review patterns later without turning the log into homework.

Field What to record Why it matters
Exact timestamp Date and actual time, not just the intended day. Makes late doses, day changes, and symptom timing readable.
Compound or stack label Use one consistent name for the peptide, medication, or stack you are tracking. Keeps the history filterable and avoids duplicate naming chaos.
Dose as prescribed Record the dose exactly as directed rather than a rough memory later. Helps the timeline stay factual without turning the page into dosing advice.
Schedule status On time, late, missed, rescheduled, travel week, supply issue, or routine shift. This is often the difference between a useful review and a confusing one.
Symptoms and context Short notes for appetite, nausea, energy, sleep, HRV, stress, meals, or training. Lets you compare what happened after the entry instead of guessing a cause.
