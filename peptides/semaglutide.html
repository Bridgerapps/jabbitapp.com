What is Semaglutide?

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist that mimics the hormone glucagon-like peptide-1. It regulates appetite, slows gastric emptying, and improves insulin sensitivity. Originally developed for type 2 diabetes, it's now widely prescribed for weight management.

Important Notes

  • Only increase if tolerated: If side effects are significant, stay at current dose longer
  • Side effects: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation are common initially
  • Injection day: Pick the same day each week; many prefer weekends
