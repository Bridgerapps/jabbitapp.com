What is TB-500?

TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4) is a synthetic version of a naturally occurring peptide present in nearly all human cells. It's renowned for its ability to promote wound healing, reduce inflammation, and improve flexibility. Unlike BPC-157, TB-500 works systemically and has a much longer half-life.

Standard Dosing Protocols

Standard Protocol (Most Common)

  • Loading phase: 5-10mg per week, split into 2 injections, for 4 weeks
  • Maintenance: 2-5mg per week ongoing
  • Injection site: Subcutaneous (any location)

Conservative Protocol

  • Dose: 2mg twice weekly (4mg total)
  • No loading phase needed
  • Good for: General wellness, mild recovery needs

The BPC-157 + TB-500 Stack

This is arguably the most popular peptide combination for injury recovery. The two work through different mechanisms:

  • BPC-157: Localized healing, angiogenesis, gut protection
  • TB-500: Systemic recovery, cell migration, inflammation reduction
  • Together: Comprehensive tissue repair at local and systemic levels

Sample Combined Protocol

Daily: BPC-157 250-500mcg

Twice weekly: TB-500 2.5-5mg

Duration: 4-8 weeks, then reassess
