What is BPC-157?

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a synthetic peptide derived from a protective protein found in human gastric juice. It has gained significant attention for its remarkable ability to accelerate healing in muscles, tendons, ligaments, and even the gut lining.

Standard Dosing Protocols

Systemic Healing Protocol

  • Dose: 250-500 mcg once daily
  • Injection site: Subcutaneous (belly fat) or intramuscular
  • Duration: 4-6 weeks
  • Best time: Morning or before bed

Localized Injury Protocol

  • Dose: 250-500 mcg
  • Injection site: Subcutaneous near injury site
  • Frequency: Once daily
  • Duration: Until symptom resolution (typically 2-4 weeks)

Reconstitution Guide

For a standard 5mg vial of BPC-157:

  1. Add 2mL of bacteriostatic water to the vial
  2. This creates a concentration of 2.5mg/mL (2500mcg/mL)
  3. For a 250mcg dose, inject 0.1mL (10 units on insulin syringe)
  4. For a 500mcg dose, inject 0.2mL (20 units on insulin syringe)
