Retatrutide TRANSCEND-T2D-1: the first phase 3 results

On March 19, 2026, Eli Lilly announced topline results from TRANSCEND-T2D-1, the first phase 3 trial of retatrutide, in adults with type 2 diabetes managed with diet and exercise.[1] The full results were published in The Lancet in June 2026.[2]

What the trial found

537 participants were randomized to retatrutide 4, 9, or 12 mg or placebo. At 40 weeks, HbA1c fell 1.69, 1.86, and 1.94 points versus 0.81 with placebo, and body weight fell 11.5%, 13.9%, and 15.3% versus 2.6%. Gastrointestinal events were the most frequent adverse events, and 2–5% stopped retatrutide because of adverse events. The trial was funded by Lilly.[2]

Status

Retatrutide remains investigational and is not FDA approved. Earlier phase 2 data in obesity are summarized on the retatrutide page.[3]
