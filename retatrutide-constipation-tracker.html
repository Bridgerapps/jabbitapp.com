What to log (high-signal fields)

The goal is consistency. A simple, boring log beats a detailed log you abandon.

Quick rule: pick one severity scale (0–10 or mild/moderate/severe) and keep it fixed for at least 2–3 weeks.

Injection context

  • Injection date/time (and whether it was early/late)
  • Dose (as prescribed) + whether it changed this week
  • Injection site (and rotation note)

Constipation tracking

  • Start time (when you first noticed symptoms)
  • Severity (your fixed scale)
  • Bowel movement notes (simple: “none / small / normal”; avoid over-detail)
  • Associated symptoms (bloating, cramps, reflux, nausea)
  • Context (travel, stress, sleep, meal timing, dehydration)
  • What you tried (neutral notes only — not recommendations)
Safety note: If symptoms feel severe or unusual for you, treat that as a real signal. This page is a tracking template, not medical guidance.

Pattern checks (non-medical)

  • Timing cluster: does it show up ~24–48 hours after injection?
  • Week-to-week shift: does it change on dose-change weeks?
  • Travel effect: does it reliably worsen on travel / disrupted sleep weeks?
  • Meal timing: does “late/heavy” eating correlate with worse symptoms?

When to switch to a general side-effect log

If your week includes multiple symptoms (nausea + reflux + fatigue + constipation), use the broader log so everything stays in one timeline.
