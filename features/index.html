Product overview

What Jabbit does

Jabbit is an iPhone logbook for the injections your prescriber has planned. Log each injection with its site, time and the amount you enter, keep track of vials, set reminders at the times you choose, and record your progress.

Get Jabbit on the App Store Read: injection protocol tracking

Free download • Jabbit Pro subscription required • iPhone

Jabbit records what you enter. It doesn't recommend medicines or doses. Not medical advice. Follow your prescriber.

Schedule + reminders

Set your routine and get gentle nudges — without overcomplicating it.

Vial-aware inventory

Log open dates and see the amount left, worked out from the doses you log.

Site history and notes

Site history across 14 zones, so you can see which areas you used least recently, plus your own notes.

Modelled level estimate

See a modelled level estimate from the doses you log, up to now. An estimate, not a measurement.

Progress you can see

Photos + measurements in one timeline so the routine feels real, not abstract.

Your records

Export your records to a file, or delete everything, at any time.

Where Jabbit fits

Jabbit isn't a 'health super app.' It's a simple logbook for people who want consistency: reminders, logs, vials and progress.

Weekly routines

Weekly reminders, a history of what you logged, and a calm design.

More complex routines

When routines get more complex, Jabbit stays usable: multi-step schedules, vial-aware context, and progress tracking that keeps you honest.
