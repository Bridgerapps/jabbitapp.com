Where Jabbit fits

Positioning-wise, Jabbit isn’t a “health super app.” It’s a private tracker designed for people who want consistency: reminders, logs, inventory, and progress — without sign-ups or data sharing.

Wedge: GLP‑1 injection tracking

The simplest path: weekly reminders, dose history, and a calm UI. Built for Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro/Zepbound style routines.

Moat: protocol + inventory tracking

When routines get more complex, Jabbit stays usable: multi-step schedules, vial-aware context, and progress tracking that keeps you honest.
