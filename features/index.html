Jabbit Features

Everything you need to track routines in one place (educational tools — not medical advice).

Download Jabbit on the App Store → No account required. Syncs via your iCloud.

💉 Smart Injection Tracking

Never miss a dose. Jabbit reminds you when to inject and tracks every injection with timestamp and location.

🔄 14-Zone Site Rotation

Built-in site rotation system prevents lipodystrophy and ensures optimal absorption. Visual guide shows where you've injected recently.

🧪 Vial Management

Track multiple vials, know when they're open, set expiration reminders. Perfect for those running multiple compounds.

📊 Reconstitution Calculator

Enter your peptide amount and desired dose - Jabbit calculates the exact water volume needed. No more math errors.

📈 Flexible Dosing Schedules

Support for every protocol:

  • Daily
  • Every Other Day (EOD)
  • 2x per week
  • 5-on-2-off
  • Custom cycles

🚀 Dose Titration Plans

Plan your escalation protocol in advance. Jabbit guides you through loading phases to maintenance.

🩸 Serum Level Charts

Visualize how compounds build up in your system over time. Understand the pharmacokinetics of your protocol.

📱 Progress Tracking

Log weight, measurements, photos. Track progress over time with charts and trends.

65+ compounds with half-life data, dosing guidelines, and user notes. Always know what you're taking.
