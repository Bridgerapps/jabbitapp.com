An injection reminder that keeps your log in one place

Jabbit sends reminders at the times you set, and you log each injection in the app. Your history, vials and sites sit together, so you’re not piecing it together from alarms, notes and calendar entries.

Jabbit is an injection log for iPhone: injections, vials and sites in one place.

Download on the App Store

Reminders on your schedule

You pick the days and times. Jabbit reminds you at those times. It doesn’t choose a schedule for you or suggest changes to one; that’s between you and your prescriber.

Log it when the reminder fires

Open Jabbit, record the injection, and add a note in your own words if you want one. Your entries build a simple history you can scroll back through.

Sites and vials alongside your log

Record which site you used and which vial an entry came from. Everything you enter is in one place when you want to look back. See everything Jabbit records on the features page.

Bring your history to appointments

A written record can make it easier to answer “when was your last one?” at an appointment. Jabbit shows what you entered; it doesn’t interpret it.
