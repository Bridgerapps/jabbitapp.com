Retatrutide timeline

Retatrutide is an investigational once-weekly GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptor agonist from Eli Lilly. It is not FDA approved. This timeline lists only milestones with a primary source.

June–August 2023: phase 2 results

Phase 2 results in obesity were published in the New England Journal of Medicine, with mean weight loss of 24.2% at 48 weeks on the 12 mg dose versus 2.1% on placebo.[1] A phase 2 trial in type 2 diabetes was published in The Lancet the same summer.[2]

March 19, 2026: first phase 3 topline

Lilly announced that TRANSCEND-T2D-1 met its primary and key secondary endpoints in adults with type 2 diabetes.[3]

June 2026: TRANSCEND-T2D-1 published

The full trial appeared in The Lancet: HbA1c reductions of 1.69–1.94 points and weight reductions of 11.5–15.3% at 40 weeks, versus 0.81 points and 2.6% on placebo.[4]
