What the evidence can safely say

GLP-1 receptor agonists are being studied for alcohol use disorder and reward-related behavior. A 2025 randomized clinical trial evaluated once-weekly semaglutide in adults with alcohol use disorder. That is meaningful, but it does not settle who benefits, how durable effects are, or whether observations apply to everyone taking semaglutide for diabetes or weight management.
