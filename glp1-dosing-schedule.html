GLP-1 Dosing Schedule

Complete guide to Mounjaro, Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound dosing and titration.

Mounjaro / Zepbound (Tirzepatide)

Starting: 2.5mg weekly → Week 4: 5mg weekly
Week 8: 7.5mg weekly (if tolerate 5mg)
Week 12: 10mg weekly → Week 16: 12.5mg weekly
Maintenance: 15mg weekly (or lowest effective dose)

Ozempic / Wegovy (Semaglutide)

Starting: 0.25mg weekly → Week 4: 0.5mg weekly
Week 8: 1mg weekly → Week 12: 1.7mg weekly
Week 16+: 2.4mg weekly (Wegovy maintenance)
Ozempic max: 2mg weekly

💡 Dosing Tips

  • Same day each week - Pick a day and stick to it
  • Inject on empty stomach - Morning or before bed works
  • Rotate injection sites - Thigh, abdomen, upper arm
  • Don't skip doses - If you miss, take within 4 days
  • Go up slowly - Stay at each dose 4+ weeks
