GLP-1 Food Enjoyment vs Anhedonia: What to Track

Less food noise is often a desired effect. Broader loss of pleasure is different. Tracking helps tell whether the change is limited to food reward or part of mood, libido, social, and energy changes.

Track food enjoyment, mood, and dose context in Jabbit
Safety: If low pleasure is severe, worsening, or paired with self-harm thoughts, seek urgent professional support. This is not a self-diagnosis page.
QuestionTracker field
Is it only food?Food enjoyment, food noise, fullness, nausea.
Is it broader pleasure?Hobbies, social drive, sex drive, music, exercise, motivation.
What changed nearby?Dose increase, rapid weight loss, under-eating, sleep loss, stress, alcohol, other meds.
Is it persistent?Weekly pattern, severity, improvement/worsening.

Evidence boundary

GLP-1 signaling is connected to appetite and reward biology, which is why these medicines are being studied for cravings and addiction. That does not prove a GLP-1 caused anhedonia in an individual person. A log is useful because it preserves timing and confounders.
