GLP-1 Side Effects — Track, Find Patterns, Feel Better

Common GLP-1 medication side effects and how to track them effectively.

Common GLP-1 Side Effects

🤢 Nausea

Most common. Usually hits 2-4 hours after injection. Track: timing, meal size, food type.

😴 Fatigue

Can last 1-3 days after dose. Track: sleep quality, activity level, dose timing.

🚽 Constipation

Common on higher doses. Track: water intake, fiber, movement.

💩 Diarrhea

Usually early on. Track: food triggers, timing, severity.

🫀 Reflux/Heartburn

Track: meal timing, fat content, elevation of head while sleeping.

😰 Anxiety/Irritability

Less discussed but real. Track: blood sugar, sleep, caffeine.

How to Track Side Effects Effectively

  1. Log immediately - Don't wait until end of day
  2. Note timing - Hours after dose, before/after meals
  3. Track severity - Scale of 1-10
  4. Correlate - See patterns: dose + food + sleep = symptom
  5. Share with provider - Patterns help doctors adjust
