What Happens When You Stop Wegovy? What to Track

Stopping Wegovy can change appetite, weight trend, side effects, and habits. The science-backed move is to track the transition clearly.

What studies suggest

Wegovy is semaglutide 2.4 mg. In the STEP 1 extension, people who stopped semaglutide after the treatment period regained a substantial portion of prior weight loss over the next year. That does not predict every individual, but it makes maintenance tracking worth taking seriously.
