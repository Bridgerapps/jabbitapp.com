First: don’t guess — log the timeline

This page won’t tell you what to do with your medication. It’s a structured way to record the details you’ll wish you had later.

Safety note: If you have severe symptoms (fainting, chest pain, persistent vomiting, severe dehydration, or anything that feels urgent), seek professional care.

What to log when a dose is missed or late

  • Planned injection day/time (your usual schedule)
  • Actual injection day/time (or “skipped” if you didn’t take it)
  • Reason (travel, supply, forgot, side effects, schedule change)
  • Symptoms for the next 72 hours (simple daily rating + notes)
  • Appetite + GI notes (especially nausea/constipation patterns)
  • Context (sleep, stress, alcohol, unusual meals, illness, new meds/supplements)
  • Questions for your clinician (e.g., new “anchor day”, pattern concerns)

3 common scenarios (and how to capture them clearly)

  • Late dose (same week): note the planned time, the actual time, and whether you also changed your reminder/“anchor day”.
  • Skipped dose: mark it explicitly as skipped, and log any symptoms/changes you notice anyway (the absence of a dose is part of the story).
  • Schedule shift (new injection day): log a “before/after” schedule so you can discuss the transition with a clinician (especially if you’re bouncing between days).

What to review over the next 7 days (simple checklist)

You’re looking for patterns you can describe, not perfect measurements. A boring, consistent log beats a detailed log you stop using.

  • Daily symptom score (0–10) + 1 sentence on what changed
  • GI pattern notes (nausea/constipation/diarrhea) and any triggers you suspect
  • Appetite + cravings (did anything feel noticeably different?)
  • Sleep + stress (often confounders when you’re trying to interpret symptoms)
  • New variables (illness, travel, alcohol, new meds/supplements)

Questions worth bringing to your next visit

You’re not asking the internet for dosing advice — you’re making the clinician conversation faster and more concrete.

  • Should I return to my prior injection day or transition to a new one?
  • If my reminder day changes, how should I track future doses so the timeline stays clear?
  • What symptoms should I watch most closely after a timing change?
  • Is there anything in my other meds/supplements or routine that changes how you want me to log side effects?
