Jabbit vs Shotsy: which GLP-1 tracker app is better?

Jabbit and Shotsy are both iPhone apps for logging GLP-1 injections. Shotsy is a GLP-1 medication tracker for shots and pills on iPhone and Android. Jabbit is a private iPhone log for injections, including GLP-1s and other peptides, the vials they come from, and your progress. This page is published by Jabbit, so every fact below comes from each app’s own App Store listing, privacy policy or terms, with the date we checked.

Which is better, Jabbit or Shotsy?

It depends on what you track. If you take a branded GLP-1 such as Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro or Zepbound and want calorie, protein and water logs alongside it, or you use Android, Shotsy is the better fit, and it has far more App Store ratings.[1][3][4] If you mix your own vials, log injections beyond GLP-1s, or want an app whose privacy policy rules out analytics tools from other companies and whose App Store label says “Data Not Collected,” Jabbit is the better fit.[2][5] Jabbit is iPhone-only.

When Shotsy is the better choice

  • You use an Android phone, or switch between iPhone and Android.[1][6]
  • You take a branded GLP-1 shot or pill and also want calorie, protein and water logs in the same app.[1]
  • You want an app with a long track record of reviews: 34,164 ratings on the US App Store as of October 29, 2026.[3]

When Jabbit is the better choice

  • You mix your own vials and want each one’s contents, age, expiry date and remaining amount kept in one place.[2]
  • You log injections other than GLP-1s, with reference entries that lead with each compound’s regulatory status and safety warnings.[2]
  • You want no account at all, records kept only on your iPhone, and no analytics tools from other companies.[5]

Either way, the app is a log. Doses and schedules come from your prescriber and the product label.
