Weight Maintenance After Stopping GLP-1s: What to Track

Trial-extension data shows weight regain can happen after semaglutide withdrawal. A maintenance log keeps appetite, habits, and trend changes visible before memory smooths them over.

Track maintenance after GLP-1 changes in Jabbit
Not a stopping plan: This page does not recommend stopping, tapering, spacing, or restarting medication. Medication changes should be clinician-directed.

What the science says

In the STEP 1 trial extension, participants discontinued semaglutide 2.4 mg and lifestyle intervention after 68 weeks. Over the following year, participants regained a substantial portion of prior weight loss and cardiometabolic improvements moved back toward baseline. The practical takeaway is not panic; it is that maintenance needs tracking.
